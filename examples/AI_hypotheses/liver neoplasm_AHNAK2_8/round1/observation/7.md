1. Observation Extraction:

 • Abstract 2: A circRNA (circ_0008194), derived from the AHNAK gene, is upregulated in HCC. It acts as a sponge for miR‑190a, leading to increased AHNAK expression and promotion of EMT and invasiveness.
 • Abstract 3: A genetic screen for HCC metastasis drivers identified several genes (e.g., Rac1, Actn2) where Rac1—a small GTPase known to require geranylgeranylation for membrane localization—plays a role in metastatic progression.
