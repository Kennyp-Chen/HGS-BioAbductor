1. Hypothesis Summary:
The hypothesis posits that in liver neoplasms, increased levels of the protein AHNAK2 work jointly with regulatory elements—specifically, components of the geranylgeranyl transferase enzyme complex and small GTPases—to enhance the process of protein geranylgeranylation. This modification alters the subcellular localization and activity of small GTPases, thereby intensifying signaling pathways that drive cellular migration, angiogenesis, and metastasis. The net outcome of these events is proposed to be the aggressive behavior and poorer clinical outcomes observed in liver cancer.
