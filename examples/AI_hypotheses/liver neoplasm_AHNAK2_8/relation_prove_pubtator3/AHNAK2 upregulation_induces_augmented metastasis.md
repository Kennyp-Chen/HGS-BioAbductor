# Search Strategy and Reasoning
I extracted the minimal MeSH keywords: "AHNAK2" (gene) and "Neoplasm Metastasis" (the MeSH for metastasis). I searched PubMed literature for their co-occurrence, focusing on abstracts for evidence that AHNAK2 upregulation induces or is associated with augmented metastasis. Several retrieved abstracts directly discuss AHNAK2's upregulation and its effect on metastasis in various cancers—including thyroid, lung, and adenocarcinoma—using both clinical samples and molecular/cellular assays, with large emphasis on mechanistic studies where AHNAK2 knockdown reduces, and overexpression increases, metastasis.

# literature evidence
## "AHNAK2 promotes thyroid carcinoma progression by activating the NF-kappaB pathway." (PMID: 34627772)
"AHNAK2 was found to be upregulated in thyroid carcinoma tissues, especially in metastatic thyroid carcinoma tissues... AHNAK2 knockdown inhibited thyroid carcinoma migration, invasion, and metastasis."

## "Silencing of AHNAK2 restricts thyroid carcinoma progression by inhibiting the Wnt/beta-catenin pathway." (PMID: 34374294)
"Silencing of AHNAK2 restricted the proliferation, metastasis, and epithelial-mesenchymal transition (EMT) of TC cells. AHNAK2 silencing inhibited the protein expression of beta-catenin and cyclin D1, and AHNAK2 overexpression had the opposite effects."

## "AHNAK2 is a biomarker and a potential therapeutic target of adenocarcinomas" (PMID: 36017889)
"In vivo, AHNAK2 knockdown significantly inhibits tumor progression and metastasis of various adenocarcinomas."

## "AHNAK2 Is Associated with Poor Prognosis and Cell Migration in Lung Adenocarcinoma" (PMID: 32904605)
"The AHNAK family is correlated with cell structure and migration, cardiac calcium channel signaling, and tumor metastasis. Previous studies showed AHNAK2 could promote tumor progression and cell migration in melanoma and renal clear cell carcinoma."

## "AHNAK2 promotes the progression of differentiated thyroid cancer through PI3K/AKT signaling pathway." (PMID: 36089788)
"The analysis results of the TCGA cohort indicated that the upregulation of AHNAK2 was associated with tumor size [P<0.001], lymph node metastasis [P<0.001], and disease stage [P<0.001]. ... Our study results indicate that AHNAK2 may promote metastasis and proliferation of thyroid cancer through PI3K/AKT signaling pathway."
