1. Observation extraction:
 a. Abstract 1: HDAC1 is overexpressed in a subset of human hepatocellular carcinomas (HCCs). Its inactivation in Hep3B cells leads to reduced tumor cell growth, induction of caspase‐independent autophagic cell death (via the LC3B‐II pathway), upregulation of cell cycle inhibitors (p21 and p27), suppression of cyclin D1 and CDK2, hypophosphorylation of pRb in the G1/S transition, and diminished E2F/DP1 transcriptional activity.
 b. Abstract 2: Overexpression of HDAC2 (a related deacetylase) is linked with malignant progression in HCC through mTORC1/NF-κBp50-driven transcriptional activation, establishing a feedback loop that maintains mitogenic signaling and contributes to metastasis and aggressive tumor behavior.
 c. Abstract 3: HDAC1/3 upregulation in HepG2 cells is associated with increased expression of interacting proteins (e.g., CNOT1, PFDN2/6, HMG20B), leading to enhanced cancer cell proliferation, altered cell morphology, and increased migration.

It is therefore not a novel, needed explanation for the observed phenomena.
