5. Conclusion:  
The hypothesis posits that upregulated HDAC1 interferes with viral DNA integration—thereby reducing viral antigen presentation and impairing immune responses, which would favor metastasis and angiogenesis. Although HDAC1 overexpression is observed in HCC, its established roles in cell cycle regulation and autophagy (and the dominant involvement of HDAC2 in oncogenic signaling) are already well explained, and no data support an influence on viral DNA integration or immune modulation. Therefore, while HDAC1 is undoubtedly important in HCC, alternative explanations for the observations (via cell cycle and autophagic modulation or via HDAC2 pathways) are more likely.
