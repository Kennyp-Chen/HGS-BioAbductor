The proposed experimental approaches include modern genomic tools (e.g., CRISPR-based epigenetic modulation, ChIP-seq, and integration site sequencing) to validate these mechanistic roles.

While elements of this pathway have been previously explored (e.g., the impact of epigenetic modifications in tumorigenesis and the role of HBV integration in hepatocellular carcinoma), combining these specific outcomes as parallel consequences of HDAC1 overexpression is an innovative take.

• The hypothesis distinguishes itself by suggesting a dual role for HDAC1 that integrates viral carcinogenesis and cell cycle control mechanisms in a single coherent model.

• The dual mechanistic proposal—viral integration leading to genomic instability and altered cell cycle progression—draws on known oncogenic processes in liver cancer where HBV is implicated.  

Strengths:
