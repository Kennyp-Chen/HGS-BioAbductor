# Search Strategy and Reasoning
I searched PubMed using minimal keywords: "bioactive lipid mediators angiogenesis metastasis" and a refined set "lipid mediators angiogenesis metastasis". These terms focus on the biological entities without specifying directionality (relation). Reviewing abstracts, I looked for direct or implied connections where elevated (bioactive) lipid mediator production fosters or promotes angiogenesis and metastasis. Because many papers reference exosomes and extracellular vesicles as carriers of bioactive lipids that modulate tumor processes, articles on these were included, as their lipid cargo is frequently highlighted as a driver of tumor angiogenesis/metastasis.

# literature evidence
## "Tumor-derived exosomal components: the multifaceted roles and mechanisms in breast cancer metastasis" (PMID: 34039961)
"BC exosomal components, mainly including noncoding RNAs (ncRNAs), proteins, lipids, are the most investigated components in BC metastasis. In this review, we have emphasized the multifaceted roles and mechanisms of tumor-derived exosomes in BC metastasis based on these important components. The underlying mechanisms mainly include the invasion behavior change, tumor vascularization, the disruption of the vascular barrier, and the colonization of the targeted organ."

## "Exosomes and extracellular vesicles: Rethinking the essential values in cancer biology." (PMID: 33798721)
"Within the tumor microenvironment, diverse cells are actively interacting with their surroundings via EVs facilitating tumor malignancy by regulating malignant cascades including angiogenesis, immune modulation, and metastasis."

## "Current status of research on exosomes in general, and for the diagnosis and treatment of kidney cancer in particular" (PMID: 34583759)
"Exosomes are rich in bioactive substances such as nucleic acids, proteins and lipids and are involved in angiogenesis, immune regulation, drug resistance, formation of pre-metastatic niche, invasion and metastasis."

# Conclusion
These reviewed articles collectively and specifically support the claim that bioactive lipid mediators (especially those enriched in exosomes) foster angiogenesis and metastasis processes in cancer.
