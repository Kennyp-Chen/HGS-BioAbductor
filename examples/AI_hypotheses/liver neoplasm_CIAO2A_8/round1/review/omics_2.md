Hypothesis Summary:
The hypothesis proposes that CIAO2A collaborates synergistically with other mRNA export regulators, specifically NXF1 and ALYREF, to enhance the overall efficiency of the mRNA export machinery. This upregulation is suggested to promote the proper nuclear export and subsequent translation of mRNAs, particularly those encoding tumor suppressor proteins. An efficient export system, therefore, may lead to elevated levels of these proteins and a simultaneous reduction in the expression or activity of factors that drive angiogenesis and metastasis—ultimately resulting in improved prognosis in liver neoplasms. Conversely, lowered expression of CIAO2A is hypothesized to disrupt this export process, potentially leading to mislocalized mRNAs, dysregulated protein synthesis, and enhanced tumor progression.
