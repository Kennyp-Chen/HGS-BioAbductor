1. Hypothesis Summary  
─────────────────────────────  
The hypothesis posits that an increased expression of CIAO2A, in coordination with nuclear mRNA export regulators such as NXF1 and XPO1, enhances the efficiency and robustness of the mRNA export machinery. Conversely, reduced expression of these components is suggested to impair mRNA export, leading to the accumulation of unprocessed transcripts, which in turn may facilitate liver cancer progression.
