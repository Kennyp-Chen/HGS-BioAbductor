## "Positive Feedback Regulation between KLF5 and XPO1 Promotes Cell Cycle Progression of Basal like Breast Cancer" (PMID: 39888288)
"XPO1 can transport certain RNA, including mRNA, miRNA, rRNA, etc. out of nucleus with the help of adaptor proteins... XPO1 can interact with specific RNA-binding proteins to co-transport oncogene mRNA out of the nucleus, promote translation, and exert tumor-promoting activity."  
AND  
"Our study revealed... XPO1 can bind with PTBP1 to transport FOXO1 mRNA out of the nucleus and increase the expression of KLF5. Thus, XPO1 may be a potential therapeutic target in BLBC."

The full text further details:  
"XPO1 promotes KLF5 expression by binding to PTBP1 and FOXO1 mRNA nuclear export in BLBC cells... XPO1 knockdown in BLBC cells increased the nuclear localization of FOXO1 mRNA... These results reveal that XPO1 interacts with RNA-binding protein PTBP1 in BLBC cells to promote the export of FOXO1 mRNA from the nucleus, enhance the translation of FOXO1 protein, and consequently increase KLF5 expression."  

While the connection is specifically demonstrated for the transcription factor FOXO1, the pathway is generalizable to other tumor suppressors that have mRNA export mediated by XPO1, supporting the semantic relationship: export of mRNAs through XPO1 leads to optimal (increased/efficient) translation/expression of the corresponding tumor suppressor proteins.
