────────────────────────────  
The hypothesis proposes that in liver neoplasms, an upregulation of GEMIN5 interferes with the double-strand break repair mechanism—specifically through break-induced replication—by interacting with and downregulating key DNA repair regulators such as ATM and BRCA1.
