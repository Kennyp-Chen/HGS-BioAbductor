## "Chromosomal instability and a deregulated cell cycle are intrinsic features of high-risk gastrointestinal stromal tumours with a metastatic potential" (PMID: 37622176)
"Our study shows that increased chromosomal instability, including chromothripsis and deregulated kinetochore and cell cycle signalling, separates high-risk samples according to metastatic potential. The increased chromosomal instability seems to be an intrinsic feature for tumours that metastasise and should be further validated as a novel prognostic biomarker for high-risk GIST."
