# literature evidence
## "Ectopic RNF168 expression promotes break-induced replication-like DNA synthesis at stalled replication forks" (PMID: 32182354)
"Mechanistically, RNF168 overexpression recruited RAD18 to ub-H2AX at HU-induced DNA breaks. Subsequently, a RAD18-SLF1 axis was responsible for initiating DNA synthesis in a manner that also required the break-induced replication (BIR) factors RAD52 and POLD3. Strikingly, the presence of wild-type BRCA1 blocked RNF168-induced DNA synthesis. Notably, BIR-like repair has previously been linked with tandem duplication events found in BRCA1-mutated genomes. Thus, in the absence of BRCA1, excessive RNF168 expression may drive BIR, and contribute to the mutational signatures observed in BRCA1-mutated cancers."

This statement directly supports that when high-fidelity homologous recombination (requiring BRCA1) is compromised, there is an increase (promotion) in error-prone BIR events at sites of replication stress.

Answer: True
