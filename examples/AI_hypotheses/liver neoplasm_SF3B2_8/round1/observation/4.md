• Observation 3: SF3B4 overexpression in early HCC contributes to aberrant splicing (e.g., of the tumor suppressor gene KLF4), leading to cell cycle deregulation and providing a diagnostic biomarker for early disease.

The hypothesis that elevated SF3B2 produces aberrant splicing of p53 and APOBEC3B, thereby enhancing viral integration and contributing to genomic instability, is not directly supported by the observations.
