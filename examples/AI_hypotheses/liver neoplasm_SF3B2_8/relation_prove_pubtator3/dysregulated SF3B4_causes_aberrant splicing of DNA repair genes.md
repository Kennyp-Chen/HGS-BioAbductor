# literature evidence
## "SF3B4 promotes ovarian cancer progression by regulating alternative splicing of RAD52", PMID: 35210412
**Supporting Quote:**  
"Importantly, we revealed that RAD52 was a potential target of SF3B4 through alternative splicing events analysis. Loss of SF3B4 led to decreased expression of RAD52, owing to intron 8 retention and generation of premature termination codons...In conclusion, our results suggested that SF3B4...promoted OC progression through effective splicing of RAD52."

And further,  
"A Sashimi plot was used to visualize the RNA-seq reads of RAD52, and intron 8 retention of RAD52 was found to be obviously increased after SF3B4 knockdown in HEY cells...RT-PCR was performed...and we found that knockdown of SF3B4 significantly increased the unspliced transcript variant and reduced the spliced transcript variant...In conclusion, SF3B4 regulates RAD52 expression through regulating intron retention."

**Semantic connection:**  
This establishes that dysregulated (knockdown or loss of function) SF3B4 directly causes aberrant splicing (specifically intron retention) of the DNA repair gene RAD52, a well-validated DNA repair factor.

Answer: True
