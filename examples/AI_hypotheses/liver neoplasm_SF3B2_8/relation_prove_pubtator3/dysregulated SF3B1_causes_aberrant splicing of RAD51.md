# Analysis
While the article uncovers that SF3B1 mutations (dysregulation) cause homologous recombination (HR) defects characterized by abnormal Rad51 function (persistent Rad51 foci after DNA damage), it specifies that there are no appreciable changes in the expression of key HR proteins such as Rad51 in SF3B1 mutant cells. However, alternative 3' splice site (A3SS) usage and skipped exons (SE) were observed in a large number of genes involved in HR, but RAD51 is not directly stated as being misspliced. The phenotype is more likely due to cumulative effects on the HR pathway components, of which RAD51 functional dynamics (persistence of foci) are abnormal, but not directly due to aberrant splicing of RAD51 specifically. Thus, no direct evidence supports that dysregulated SF3B1 causes aberrant splicing of RAD51.
