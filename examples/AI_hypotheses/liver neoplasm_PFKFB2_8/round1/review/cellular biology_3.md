It further speculates that upregulated PFKFB2 could either directly enhance or trigger a compensatory downregulation of key enzymes within the geranylgeranylation pathway. This alteration in lipid modification of signaling proteins is suggested to affect cellular adhesion and migration, thereby facilitating metastasis and angiogenesis. The underlying idea is that a cross‐talk mechanism between PFKFB2 and modulators of the geranylgeranylation pathway represents a novel link between metabolic reprogramming and aggressive tumor behavior in liver cancer.
