This aberrant post-translational modification is suggested to activate signaling cascades that contribute to increased cell motility and angiogenesis. The hypothesis also outlines a plan to validate the mechanistic link using advanced techniques such as CRISPR-based gene modulation, single-cell RNA sequencing, and metabolic flux analysis.
