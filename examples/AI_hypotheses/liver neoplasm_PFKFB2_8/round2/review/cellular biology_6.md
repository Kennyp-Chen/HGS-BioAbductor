1. Hypothesis Summary

This is proposed to occur either by upregulating or through direct physical interactions with geranylgeranyltransferases. As a result, key signaling proteins, especially small GTPases, undergo increased geranylgeranylation, thereby amplifying metastatic and angiogenic signaling pathways. The research plan includes the use of advanced gene-editing methods (such as CRISPR), in addition to comprehensive proteomic, metabolomic, and functional assays to determine whether PFKFB2 directly interacts with or transcriptionally influences enzymes involved in geranylgeranylation, and to investigate whether other metabolic regulators further synergize within this cascade.
