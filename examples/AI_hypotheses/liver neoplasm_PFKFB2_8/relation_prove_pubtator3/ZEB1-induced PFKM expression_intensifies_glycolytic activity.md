## "ZEB1 enhances Warburg effect to facilitate tumorigenesis and metastasis of HCC by transcriptionally activating PFKM" (PMID: 33897890)
**Exact sentence from the abstract:**
"ZEB1 transcriptionally upregulates the expression of the muscle isoform of phosphofructokinase-1 (PFKM), a rate-limiting enzyme in glycolysis... Silencing of ZEB1 in MHCC-97H and HCC-LM3 cell leads to impaired PFKM expression, glycolysis, proliferation and invasion, and such impairments are rescued by exogenous expression of PFKM."

This directly supports that ZEB1-induced PFKM expression intensifies glycolytic activity, as the decrease in both PFKM and glycolysis caused by ZEB1 knockdown is reversed by restoring PFKM.
