Such modifications are hypothesized to drive oncogenic processes such as enhanced cell migration, angiogenesis, and metastasis. The hypothesis further indicates that these metabolic changes are detectable and can be validated using integrated methodologies like CRISPR gene editing, metabolic fluxomics, and single-cell proteomics.
