• The hypothesis further suggests that RMDN1’s activity is modulated through interactions with other chromatin modifiers such as KDM3A and NSD1.  
• Advanced methodologies (CRISPR-based epigenetic modifications, single-cell ATAC-seq, and quantitative proteomics) are proposed to test these relationships and the impact of RMDN1 on liver neoplasm prognosis.
