It is essential to demonstrate that the changes in chromatin state relate directly to the downregulation of oncogenes linked to metastasis and angiogenesis.

The hypothesis claims that upregulation of RMDN1 enforces a tumor-suppressive state. It would be beneficial to clarify whether RMDN1 upregulation is a compensatory response in tumor cells or a driver event that actively represses an oncogenic program.
