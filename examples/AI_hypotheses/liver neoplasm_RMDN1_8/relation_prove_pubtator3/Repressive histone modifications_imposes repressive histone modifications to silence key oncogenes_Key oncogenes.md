# literature evidence
## "Broad genic repression domains signify enhanced silencing of oncogenes" (PMID: 33144558)
Exact supporting sentence from the abstract:
"A BGRD is a widespread enrichment domain of the repressive histone modification H3K27me3 and is further enriched with multiple other repressive marks including H3K9me3, H3K9me2, and H3K27me2. ... Therefore, BGRDs play key roles in epigenetic regulation of cancer and provide a direction for mutation-independent discovery of oncogenes."  
From the full text:
"Several other repressive histone modifications, e.g., H3K27me2, H3K9me2, and H3K9me3, showed similar widespread enrichment in the BGRDs defined by widespread H3K27me3. ... Therefore, BGRDs in CD4+ T cell were associated with enhanced repression of oncogenes, but not with other silent genes."

Answer: True
