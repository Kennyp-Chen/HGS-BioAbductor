• The hypothesis also outlines a technological framework (employing CRISPR screens and single-cell RNA sequencing) aimed at functionally validating the mechanism and exploring tumor microenvironment heterogeneity.  
• An intriguing aspect of the hypothesis is the suggestion that in this specific context, HDAC1 may transition from its traditionally oncogenic role to a mediator of tumor suppression.
