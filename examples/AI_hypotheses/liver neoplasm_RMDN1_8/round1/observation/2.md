1. Observation extraction:
 • Abstract 1 shows that reduced expression of the RNA‐binding protein RBM38 leads to disruption of the p53‐mdm2 loop (with increased mdm2 and decreased wild‐type p53), thereby promoting hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC).  • Abstract 2 demonstrates that RBM43, another RNA‐binding protein, functions as a tumor suppressor in HCC by modulating Cyclin B1 mRNA and affecting cell‐cycle progression.

However, the observed phenomena (RBM38’s impact on the p53-mdm2 loop, RBM43’s regulation of CyclinB1, and the HDAC2 feedback loop) are all explained by their own established regulatory pathways. The hypothesis does not bridge a gap in explaining any subset of these observations; rather, it suggests an alternative mechanism that has not yet been linked to these particular processes.

However, they already have detailed, alternative causal explanations that make it less likely that RMDN1’s modulation of chromatin binding is the primary or novel driver for these effects.
