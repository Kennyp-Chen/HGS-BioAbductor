Conversely, when RMDN1 is downregulated, the loss of this inhibitory effect results in a more open chromatin configuration that facilitates oncogenic transcription, promoting tumor progression. Additionally, the hypothesis indicates that other regulatory genes may cooperate with RMDN1 to further influence the enzymatic activities involved in chromatin modifications, affecting cancer prognosis.
