1. Observation Extraction:
 • Observation 1: HCC tumors exhibit a highly immunosuppressive microenvironment largely driven by tumor‐associated macrophages (TAMs), which are predominantly in the M2 phenotype that impairs immunotherapy efficacy.
 • Observation 2: The biophysical properties of the tumor microenvironment—especially extracellular matrix (ECM) stiffness—affect cell signaling; stiffer substrates activate integrin/FAK signaling and related pathways in HCC cells.
 • Observation 3: ECM stiffness influences the activation of latent TGF‐β1 via cytoskeletal tension and upregulates integrin-associated proteins (e.g., β1 integrin, FAK, Smad2) in HCC, which are implicated in tumor progression.
 • Observation 4: There is growing evidence that incorporating TME features (including ECM characteristics) into cancer models and therapies is essential to better replicate and target the complex interactions in HCC.

2. Causal Analysis (Individual):

 Observation 1:

 Observation 4:
