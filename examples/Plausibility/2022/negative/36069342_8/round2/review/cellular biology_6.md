1. Hypothesis Summary  
The hypothesis proposes a multi-pronged strategy to reprogram tumor‐associated macrophages (TAMs) in hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC). It suggests that by (a) modulating extracellular matrix (ECM) stiffness using advanced, tunable biomaterials; (b) applying nanoparticle-mediated, targeted inhibition of the integrin‐β5/FAK/MEK/ERK signaling cascade (which stabilizes HIF1α); and (c) employing real-time mechanobiological feedback via advanced imaging and computational models, one can shift TAMs from an immunosuppressive M2 state to an immunostimulatory M1 state. This reprogramming is expected to enhance cytotoxic T cell infiltration and synergize with current immunotherapies to suppress tumor progression.
