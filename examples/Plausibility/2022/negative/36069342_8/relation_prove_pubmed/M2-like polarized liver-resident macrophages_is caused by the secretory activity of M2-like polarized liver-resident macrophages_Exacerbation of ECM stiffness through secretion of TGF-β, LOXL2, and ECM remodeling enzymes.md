## "Computational/experimental evaluation of liver metastasis post hepatic injury: interactions with macrophages and transitional ECM." (PMID: 31636296)
"The complex interactions between subclinical changes to hepatic extracellular matrix (ECM) in response to injury and tumor-associated macrophage microenvironmental cues facilitating metastatic cell seeding remain poorly understood... circulating monocytes and resident macrophages are recruited by tumors to facilitate neoangiogenesis and proliferation. In turn, macrophage signaling and ECM turnover both in cancer and in liver diseases involve macrophage modulation of the microenvironment, partly mediated by altered macrophage polarization... In the context of tumor associated macrophages (TAMs), ... stromal macrophages bordering hypoxic regions, exhibit a more anti-inflammatory ('alternative' M2) trophic phenotype. These M2-like TAMs release pro-angiogenic factors such as vascular endothelial growth factor (VEGF) ... Macrophage polarization was simulated to occur in the tumor microenvironment based on the levels of cytokines present therein, with TNF-α and TGFβ1 being representative molecules influencing M1 and M2 polarization, respectively... Tumor growth is modulated by ECM density: ... ECM remodeling due to normal wound healing as well as liver diseases employs developmental pathways highly associated with cancer progression... ECM density for the tECM reflected the increased collagens and ECM proteins determined to comprise the transitional matrix... Degradation is further modulated by density M of matrix degrading enzymes (MDEs), released by proliferating tumor cells and vascular endothelial cells to remodel the ECM. ... M2 growth factor positively affects the proliferating region ... M2 macrophages can also stimulate the proliferation of hypoxic tumor tissue, which is simulated to occur at lower rates than in proliferating tissue. The M2 macrophage-secreted tumor growth factor concentration, F ... can transiently lower the local viable oxygen threshold... Both primary tumor simulations had larger necrosis than metastatic tumor simulations, which had mostly hypoxic cores. Accordingly, primary tumor tissue tended toward a balance between M1 and M2 populations, while metastatic tumors had a higher infiltration of M1 subtypes, which would result in growth restriction relative to all other cases."

*While this paper establishes that M2-polarized (and likely liver-resident) macrophages are functionally tied to TGF-β production and ECM remodeling in the liver, and that these processes can affect ECM characteristics, it does not provide direct experimental evidence for secretion of LOXL2 or tie specific ECM remodeling enzyme secretion to increased liver ECM stiffness specifically.*

## "Uncovering a Novel Functional Interaction Between Adult Hepatic Progenitor Cells, Inflammation and EGFR Signaling During Bile Acids-Induced Injury." (PMID: 38725853)
"For HPC-macrophage interaction analysis, we tested the effects of HPC-conditioned medium on mouse peritoneal macrophages, specifically, a potential effect on M1- and M2-like phenotype. ... Altogether these data evidence a relevant role for HPC in the hepatic cellular interactome through the secretion of a plethora of factors that contribute to HSC activation and regulation of macrophage polarity... Upon treatment with bile acids and EGF HPC secretome drastically changes, ... switching macrophage polarization to M2-like phenotype. ... important changes were observed in the secretome of treated HPC in relation with inflammation, with an enrichment in inflammatory cell-recruiting factors (CXCL5, SECRETED PHOSPHOPROTEIN 1 (SPP1) or TIMP1), macrophage M2-inducing factors (CSF1, GALECTIN 1 (LGALS1), CCN4, CXCL16) or factors interfering with M1-like macrophage polarization (CLU) ... presence of neutrophils recruitment and degranulation factors (ADAM10, TIMP1, CXCL5). ... In summary, this work contributes to clarify HPC response under a cholestatic damage. Notably, we have elucidated a novel contribution of HPC in the pathophysiology of the cholestatic liver disease. ... we evidence that HPC, through the secretion of growth factors, cytokines, chemokines and ECM assembly and remodeling proteins, contribute to remodel the microenvironment relevantly impacting on the regenerative process by directly modulating other hepatic cells phenotype and functional properties."

*This article provides evidence that liver progenitor cells modulate macrophage polarization and secrete ECM remodeling proteins, but it lacks direct demonstration that M2-polarized, liver-resident macrophages secrete LOXL2 and TGF-β in a way that directly causes ECM stiffening.*

# Conclusion

Both the best available primary literature and computational/ex-vivo models demonstrate that M2-like liver macrophages can secrete TGF-β and potentially contribute to profibrotic signaling and ECM remodeling in the liver, sometimes acting via inducing factors and the activation of other cell types (e.g., hepatic stellate cells). However, neither the abstracts nor the full texts reviewed contain a direct causal demonstration that the secretory activity of M2-like polarized liver-resident macrophages alone exacerbates ECM stiffness through LOXL2, TGF-β, and ECM remodeling enzyme secretion in vivo.
