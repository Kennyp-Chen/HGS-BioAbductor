# Search Strategy and Reasoning
Initial searches using all key pathway components and liver-resident macrophages as keywords did not yield relevant results. I then relaxed the keywords stepwise: first broadening "liver-resident macrophages" to "macrophage polarization", then stepping down the pathway keywords from "Integrin β5/FAK/MEK/ERK" to "Integrin AND FAK AND ERK AND macrophage polarization." This finally led to publications discussing FAK/ERK signaling and macrophage function/polarization in general. I did not find any abstract that directly links integrin β5/FAK/MEK/ERK activation in liver-resident macrophages to M2-like immunosuppressive polarization. The two closest abstracts discuss FAK-dependent signaling in macrophages but not their M2-polarization nor specifically in liver-resident subsets.
