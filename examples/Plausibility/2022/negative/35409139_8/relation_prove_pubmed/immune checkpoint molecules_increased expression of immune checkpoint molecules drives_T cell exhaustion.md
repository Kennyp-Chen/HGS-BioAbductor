# Search Strategy and Reasoning
I used the combined keywords "immune checkpoint molecules" AND "T cell exhaustion" to specifically target papers addressing the molecular relationship between immune checkpoint molecule expression and the process or phenotype of T cell exhaustion, in particular seeking any semantic evidence that increased expression of these molecules drives or is central to T cell exhaustion. The first round of abstract review focused on whether there is a direct or implied causal connection, as required by the relationship.
