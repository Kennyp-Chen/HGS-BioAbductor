1. Hypothesis Summary

The hypothesis proposes that in hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) cells, reduced expression (downregulation) of TGF‐βR3 leads to an increased production of complement component C5a. This elevation in C5a, in turn, promotes epithelial-to-mesenchymal transition (EMT) and upregulates immune checkpoint molecules, both of which contribute to tumor aggressiveness and immune evasion. Consequently, targeting or inhibiting the TGF‐βR3/C5a signaling axis may decrease tumor invasiveness and restore effective anti-tumor immunity in HCC.
