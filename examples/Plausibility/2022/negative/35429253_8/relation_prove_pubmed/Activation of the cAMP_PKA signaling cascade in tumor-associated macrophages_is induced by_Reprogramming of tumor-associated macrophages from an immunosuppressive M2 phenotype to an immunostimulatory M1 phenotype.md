# Search Strategy and Reasoning
I searched for literature connecting "cAMP/PKA signaling," "tumor-associated macrophages," and their polarization state (M1 vs M2). The abstract for PMID: 38794797 specifically addresses the activation of cAMP signaling in tumor-associated macrophages (TAMs) and its effect on polarization towards the M2 phenotype, which is immunosuppressive. If cAMP signaling promotes M2 phenotype, then, by implication, inhibition or downregulation of cAMP/PKA could facilitate M1 (immunostimulatory) reprogramming. However, to directly support that *activation* of cAMP/PKA signaling *induces* reprogramming from M2 to M1, I needed explicit evidence, ideally showing conversion or reprogramming towards M1 upon pathway activation.

# literature evidence
## "Pumilio RNA binding family member 1 deficiency activates anti-tumor immunity in hepatocellular carcinoma via restraining M2 macrophage polarization." PMID: 38794797
Exact sentence from abstract: "We also showed that PUM1 promotes the transformation of TAMs into pro-tumorigenic M2-like phenotypes by activating cAMP signaling pathway."

However, this supports the opposite direction: activation of cAMP signaling induces M2, not M1, polarization. There is no evidence in the abstract or surrounding literature that cAMP/PKA activation reprograms TAMs from M2 (immunosuppressive) to M1 (immunostimulatory).

Answer: False
