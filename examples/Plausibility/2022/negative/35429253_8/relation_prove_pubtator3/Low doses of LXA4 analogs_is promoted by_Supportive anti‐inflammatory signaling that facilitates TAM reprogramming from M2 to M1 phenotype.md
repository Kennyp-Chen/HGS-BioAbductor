## "Specialized Pro-Resolving Mediators Mitigate Cancer-Related Inflammation: Role of Tumor-Associated Macrophages and Therapeutic Opportunities" (PMID: 34276698)
Exact sentence from full text:
"In the late 1990’s, Serhan and colleagues designed the first LXA4 analogs with chemical modifications of native LXA4, resulting in increased stability due to enzyme conversion resistance. ... Importantly, these analogs demonstrated similar biological activities than native SPMs in vitro and in vivo. They were able to stimulate macrophages pro-resolving functions and induce inflammation resolution in a GPCR-dependent manner in in vitro and in vivo inflammatory models. These analogs also exerted antitumor effects, including inhibition of primary tumor growth and metastasis as previously detailed in Tables 2 and 3. Of note, analogs displayed biological activities at doses lower than native SPMs. ... Importantly, these studies showed that SPMs mediate their antitumor actions in part by counteracting pro-tumorigenic properties of TAMs, including stimulation of tumor cell proliferation and migration, epithelial-mesenchymal transition (EMT), angiogenesis and production of tumor growth-supportive cytokines and chemokines. As mentioned above, SPMs altered TAMs' phenotype both in vitro and in vivo and stimulated apoptotic tumor cell clearance."
