These two processes are thought to act synergistically, ultimately inducing apoptosis in CD4⁺ T cells. The loss of these immune cells is hypothesized to impair tumor immunosurveillance, thereby creating a microenvironment that favors hepatocarcinogenesis (liver cancer development).
