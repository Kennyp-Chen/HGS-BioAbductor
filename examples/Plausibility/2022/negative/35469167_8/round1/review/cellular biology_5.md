──────────────────────────────
The hypothesis states that in hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC), a disruption of lipid homeostasis results in elevated levels of C16‐ceramide. This increase in C16‐ceramide, in turn, stabilizes YAP/TAZ signaling and suppresses p53 activity. Together, these events promote tumor cell proliferation and enable immune escape. Based on this, the hypothesis proposes that targeting the effects of C16‐ceramide on YAP/TAZ could serve as a novel therapeutic strategy for HCC.
