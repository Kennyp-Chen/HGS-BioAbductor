1. Observation extraction:
 • Abstract 1: In NAFLD-associated HCC, dysregulated sphingolipid metabolism is crucial. Sphingosine kinase 2 (SphK2) drives tumorigenesis by regulating ceramide transfer protein (CERT) and shifting the balance between pro‐cancer sphingomyelin and anti‐cancer ceramide.
 • Abstract 2: In HCC, SIRT5 deficiency leads to heightened bile acid production, which in turn promotes M2‐like macrophage polarization. This creates an immunosuppressive tumor microenvironment that favors cancer initiation.
 • Abstract 3: Metabolic reprogramming, particularly abnormal lipid metabolism in the liver, is a key contributor to HCC progression and is being targeted therapeutically.

Therefore, while the hypothesis might account for some reduction in pro‐oncogenic signaling, it does not provide a novel explanation for a subset of the observations because alternative mechanisms are already better supported by the data.
