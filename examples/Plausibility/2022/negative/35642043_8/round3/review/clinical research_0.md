1. Hypothesis Summary:

Additionally, real-time metabolic monitoring is proposed to dynamically adjust CAR-T cell activity, with the overall goal of considerably enhancing tumor cell lysis and therapeutic efficacy.

The multidimensional framework aims to address several intrinsic mechanisms of tumor resistance concurrently, distinguishing it from more conventional single-target modifications.
