# Search Strategy and Reasoning
The relationship being tested is whether 'Tumor microenvironment cytokines (IL-6, TNF-α)' can activate 'PTM enzyme JAK1'. The minimal MeSH terms extracted for search were "IL-6 JAK1" and "TNF-α JAK1". I searched PubTator for abstracts explicitly mentioning both entities together and reviewed their contexts for explicit or implied activation.

The search yielded multiple results for "IL-6 JAK1", but none for "TNF-α JAK1". The abstracts were reviewed for evidence of JAK1 activation by cytokines, specifically IL-6. One article directly describes the activation of JAK1 by IL-6 in the context of tumor-related and fibrotic processes, and another in the context of cancer glycolysis and migration, both fitting the 'tumor microenvironment cytokine' descriptor.
