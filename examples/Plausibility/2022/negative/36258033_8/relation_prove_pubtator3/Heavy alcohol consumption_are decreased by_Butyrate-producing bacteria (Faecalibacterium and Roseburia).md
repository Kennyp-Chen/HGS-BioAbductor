# Search Strategy and Reasoning
Minimal MeSH terms are: "Alcohol consumption" AND "Faecalibacterium" AND "Roseburia". I first searched for literature co-mentioning heavy alcohol use and these specific butyrate-producing genera. I then narrowed focus to direct studies of how alcohol consumption alters their abundance. I prioritized original studies or systematic reviews with gut microbiota profiling in alcohol-consuming human cohorts or relevant animal models.

I reviewed abstracts for evidence that heavy alcohol use decreases Faecalibacterium and Roseburia, and then examined full texts of key results, particularly PMID: 35280887 and PMID: 39488100, since their abstracts indicated direct, quantitative analysis of these bacteria in the context of alcohol exposure or alcohol use disorder.
