1. Hypothesis Summary

The hypothesis posits that individuals carrying the PNPLA3 risk variant are predisposed to alcohol-induced gut dysbiosis. Together, these effects are hypothesized to accelerate the progression from alcoholic cirrhosis to hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC).
