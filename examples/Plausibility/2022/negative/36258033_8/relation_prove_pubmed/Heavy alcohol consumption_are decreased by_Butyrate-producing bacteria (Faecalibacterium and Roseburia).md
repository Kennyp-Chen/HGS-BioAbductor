# Search Strategy and Reasoning
I searched PubMed with the keywords: "alcohol consumption AND Faecalibacterium AND Roseburia." These represent the minimal set of MeSH-style functional terms required to assess whether heavy alcohol consumption decreases the abundance of the main butyrate-producing bacteria: Faecalibacterium and Roseburia. The resulting abstracts were scanned for semantic links, in particular, evidence showing a decrease in these bacteria associated with alcohol use. One article (PMID: 35280887) specifically studied gut microbiome differences in individuals with varying levels of alcohol consumption and analyzed Faecalibacterium prausnitzii and Roseburia hominis.
