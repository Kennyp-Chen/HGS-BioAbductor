# Search Strategy and Reasoning
The initial keyword search was "IL-6 STAT3 liver fibrosis" to find direct links in the context of inflammation and fibrosis. As abstracts were reviewed, there was evidence in related inflammatory liver diseases. To refine the evidence specifically for the inflammatory cascade, the query was expanded to "IL-6 STAT3 inflammation liver fibrosis," aiming to capture mechanistic connections (cascade/inflammatory response) in the development of liver fibrosis. The key result—PMC10461951 (PMID: 37256725)—is a study on the IL-6/STAT3 pathway in liver inflammation and fibrosis, and was selected for full text review since the abstract noted IL-6-dependent STAT3 activation as "pivotal steps in PSC pathogenesis", and PSC is a fibrotic liver disease.
