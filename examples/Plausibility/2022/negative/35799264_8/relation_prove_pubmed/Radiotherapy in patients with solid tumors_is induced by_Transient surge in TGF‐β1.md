# literature evidence
## "TGF‐β mediated drug resistance in solid cancer." PMID: 37100675
Abstract sentence: "TGF-β signaling has been found to be strongly activated in cancers after treatment with chemotherapeutic agents and radiotherapy, resulting in the onset of drug resistance conditions."

Full text explanation: The discussion clarifies that TGF-β family signaling (including TGF-β1) is rapidly and strongly induced by radiotherapy in solid tumors, which drives several resistance and adaptation phenomena. The text states: "TGF-β signaling… is strongly activated in cancers after treatment with chemotherapeutic agents and radiotherapy, resulting in onset of drug resistance... Overactivation of TGF-β will activate the anti-apoptotic pathway via Smad... Overactivation of TGF-β1 supports proliferation, invasion and chemoresistance." This supports an induced and dynamic (surge) increase post-radiotherapy.

Answer: True
