# Search Strategy and Reasoning
My initial search combined the minimal MeSH-representative terms: "nanoparticles AND immunomodulatory agents AND doxorubicin AND siRNA AND TOX AND TGF-beta". This yielded no results, so I refined to "nanoparticles AND doxorubicin AND siRNA AND TGF-beta" to capture core mechanistic relationships involving nanoparticle delivery, doxorubicin, siRNAs, and a key immunosuppressive signaling molecule (TGF-beta). The goal was to find any evidence that nanoparticle-mediated delivery can counter downstream inhibitory signals (in particular, via doxorubicin and siRNAs against TGF-beta or genes like TOX).
I focused on abstracts which describe:
- a nanoparticle delivery system carrying doxorubicin and siRNA,
- counteraction of downstream inhibitory signals (such as TGF-beta),
- and, if possible, explicit mention of immunomodulation.

Two abstracts were relevant. PMID 39663738 discusses a nanoparticle platform co-delivering doxorubicin and siRNA targeting Snail (with effects on the TGF-beta pathway), and describes combined targeting of tumor cells and the suppressive tumor microenvironment. PMID 34270987 reviews how nanoparticle delivery of doxorubicin and siRNAs can reverse EMT (downstream of TGF-beta), but does not directly cite TOX or combine all elements as specifically as PMID 39663738.
