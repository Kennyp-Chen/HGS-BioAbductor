1. Observation extraction:
 • Observation 1: A carrier‐free nanoparticle system delivering doxorubicin, melittin, and anti‐TOX siRNA produced enhanced immunogenic cell death (ICD), which increased CD8⁺ T‐cell infiltration while mitigating T‐cell exhaustion in liver cancer and metastasis.
