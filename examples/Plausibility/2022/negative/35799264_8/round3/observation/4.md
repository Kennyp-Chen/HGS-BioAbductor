1. Observation extraction:
 • In Abstract 1, a carrier‐free nanoparticle delivering doxorubicin, melittin, and anti‐TOX siRNA produced enhanced immunogenic cell death, increased CD8⁺ T‐cell infiltration, and reduced T‐cell exhaustion in liver cancer and metastasis.

 • In Abstract 3, radiofrequency ablation (RFA) combined with siRNA targeting PD‐1 and TGF‐β resulted in reduced tumor viability, increased apoptosis, enhanced CD8⁺ T‐cell activity and inflammatory cytokines, and suppression of the PI3K/AKT/mTOR pathway.

3. Causal analysis (summary):
 Would we see some of the observations if the hypothesis was true: If radiotherapy induces a transient TGF‐β1 increase that primes CD8⁺ T cells into a reversible pre‐exhausted state, one might expect a window for immunotherapeutic intervention that could be exploited with PD‐1 inhibitors. However, the observations from these abstracts are already explained by strategies that actively counteract immunosuppressive signals (e.g., interfering with TOX, blocking TGF‐β, or modulating immune cell populations through TACE) without relying on a transient priming mechanism. Thus, the hypothesis does not uniquely account for or add substantial novelty to the established explanations.

4. Disproof analysis:
 Does some observations disprove the hypothesis: There is no direct contradiction—none of the observations explicitly rule out transient TGF‐β1-mediated priming. However, the fact that the most effective strategies in the observations involve blocking or interfering with TGF‐β signaling (rather than harnessing its transient increase) suggests that better established explanations are already in place. This diminishes the plausibility of the hypothesis as a superior or novel causal explanation.

5. Conclusion:
 While the hypothesis maintains a core idea of timed immunotherapeutic intervention, the observations indicate that successful antitumor strategies are currently achieved by methods that either reverse T‐cell exhaustion directly (via siTOX or PD‐1 blockade) or block TGF‐β signaling. As such, the hypothesis does not provide a novel causal explanation that fills an existing gap in understanding; instead, other explanations appear more likely.
