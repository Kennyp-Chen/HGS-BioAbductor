1. Hypothesis Summary

The hypothesis posits that by inhibiting either the release or the cellular uptake of tumor-derived extracellular vesicles (EVs), one can reverse both the local and systemic immunosuppressive effects typically induced by these vesicles. As a result, this reversal would restore T cell functionality, ultimately enhancing the therapeutic efficacy of PD-1 blockade—a key immunotherapeutic strategy—in treating solid tumors.

Thus, the hypothesis offers an innovative perspective by:
 • Bridging EV biology with checkpoint inhibition.
 • Suggesting a combinatorial approach that could address an identified resistance mechanism to PD-1 therapy.
