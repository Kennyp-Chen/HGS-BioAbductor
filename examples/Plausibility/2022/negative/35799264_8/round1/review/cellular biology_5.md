1. Hypothesis Summary

The hypothesis proposes that a sequential treatment approach—first administering low-dose, fractionated radiotherapy to transiently expand a subset of precursor exhausted CD8⁺ T cells, and thereafter applying PD-1 inhibitor therapy—will convert immunologically "cold" tumors (which typically lack significant immune cell infiltration) into "hot" tumors (characterized by immune activation). This conversion is intended to enhance anti-tumor immune responses and, in turn, improve overall treatment outcomes in solid tumors such as non‐small cell lung cancer.
