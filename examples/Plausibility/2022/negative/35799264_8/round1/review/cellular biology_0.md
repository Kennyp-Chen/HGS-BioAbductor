1. Hypothesis Summary

The hypothesis proposes that combining a TGF‐β1 inhibitor with radiotherapy and PD-1 immune checkpoint blockade can “reprogram” both the systemic and tumor immune microenvironments. The key mechanism suggested is the preservation of precursor exhausted T cells while preventing these cells from progressing into terminal exhaustion. The end goals of this strategy are to enhance tumor control (anti-tumor efficacy) and to reduce the risk of hyperprogression—a paradoxical acceleration of tumor growth—specifically in cancers such as hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) and non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC).
