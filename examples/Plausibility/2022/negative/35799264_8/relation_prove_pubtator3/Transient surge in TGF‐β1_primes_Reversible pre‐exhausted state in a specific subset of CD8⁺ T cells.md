# literature evidence

## "The role of transforming growth factor-beta (TGF-beta) in the formation of exhausted CD8 + T cells" (PMID: 38884843)
"Recent studies have shown that TGF-beta is one of the drivers for the development of exhausted CD8 + T cells. In this review, we summarized the role and mechanisms of TGF-beta in the formation of exhausted CD8 + T cells and discussed ways to target those to ultimately enhance the efficacy of immunotherapy."

## "Thrombospondin-1 induces CD8+ T cell exhaustion and immune suppression within the tumor microenvironment of ovarian cancer." (PMID: 40349060)
"Functional assays demonstrated that THBS1 promotes macrophage recruitment and induces M2 polarization through TGF-beta1 and IL-4 signaling. ... This study positions THBS1 as a key regulator of the OC TME, linking TAM recruitment and polarization to CD8+ T cell exhaustion via immune checkpoint modulation."

## "ARHGAP4 promotes colon cancer metastasis through the TGF-beta signaling pathway and may be associated with T cell exhaustion." (PMID: 38805788)
"Immune infiltration analysis showed that ARHGAP4 was not only positively correlated with CD8+ T cells, but also positively correlated with T cell exhaustion markers programmed cell death 1 (PDCD-1), cytotoxic T-lymphocyte associated protein 4 (CTLA-4), and lymphocyte activating 3 (LAG-3). ... Meanwhile, the expression of TGF-beta1, p-Smad2, and p-Smad3, which are associated with the TGF-beta/Smad pathway, all decreased [after ARHGAP4 knockdown]."
