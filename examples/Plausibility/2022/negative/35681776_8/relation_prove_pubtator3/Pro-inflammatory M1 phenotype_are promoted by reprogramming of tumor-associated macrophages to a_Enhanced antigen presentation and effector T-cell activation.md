# Search Strategy and Reasoning
I first identified the minimal keyword set that includes: "M1 macrophage," "antigen presentation," "T cell activation," and "tumor-associated macrophages." These terms precisely capture the entities in the query. I searched PubTator for articles connecting M1 phenotype (pro-inflammatory macrophages, specifically reprogrammed from tumor-associated macrophages) with enhanced antigen presentation and effector T-cell activation. A secondary, more focused search combined these concepts to look for direct evidence of the relationship.

Reviewing the article "Ultrasound nanodroplets loaded with Siglec-G siRNA and Fe3O4 activate macrophages and enhance phagocytosis for immunotherapy of triple-negative breast cancer" (PMID: 39696453), the abstract alone described that re-polarizing TAMs to the M1 phenotype can help "mitigate the immunosuppressive TME" with consequences for antigen presentation and T cell activation. Full-text review further validated the specific mechanism: Fe3O4 induces repolarization of M2 (tumor-infiltrative) macrophages to pro-inflammatory M1 phenotype, which then enhances antigen presentation and facilitates tumor-specific T cell activation and proliferation. Specific data from the in vivo and in vitro experiments support the dual roles—macrophage phenotype shift and subsequent improvement in T cell activation.
