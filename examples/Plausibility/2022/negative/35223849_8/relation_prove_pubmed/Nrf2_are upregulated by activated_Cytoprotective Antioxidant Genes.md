## "Luteolin Induces Nrf2 Activity in C2C12 Cells: Implications for Muscle Health." (PMID: 40362329)
"Activation of Nrf2 enhances antioxidant defenses by upregulating genes responsible for producing antioxidant enzymes, thereby maintaining redox homeostasis and preventing oxidative distress known to be associated with muscle aging."  
AND:  
"To further verify these results, we investigated the gene expression of known Nrf2 target genes, in particular Nqo1 and Hmox1, in proliferating C2C12 cells using RT-qPCR... Our results indicate that Nqo1 expression significantly increased after 24 h of stimulation with luteolin at a concentration of 3.125 μM ... Regarding the Nrf2 target gene Hmox1, its expression was exclusively elevated after 24 h of stimulation with luteolin at concentrations of either 6.25 or 12.5 μM..."
