# Search Strategy and Reasoning
Initial keyword queries included: "Hepatic Stellate Cell Activation Attenuation of Liver Fibrosis", "Hepatic Stellate Cell Activation Liver Fibrosis", "Stellate Cell Activation Fibrosis", and "inhibition Hepatic Stellate Cell Activation fibrosis". The goal was to find abstracts directly linking the inhibition of hepatic stellate cell (HSC) activation with the attenuation (reduction/reversal) of liver fibrosis. The literature review focused on whether inhibiting HSC activation is reported as a mechanistic basis for achieving liver fibrosis attenuation.
