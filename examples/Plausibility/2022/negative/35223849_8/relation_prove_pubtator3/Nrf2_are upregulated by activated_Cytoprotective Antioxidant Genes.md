## "Maternal Calorie Restriction Induces a Transcriptional Cytoprotective Response in Embryonic Liver Partially Dependent on Nrf2" (PMID: 36421460)
"Calorie restriction during pregnancy elicits the transcriptional induction of cytoprotective/antioxidant genes in the fetal liver, which is at least partially Nrf2-dependent..."

From the Results and Discussion Sections:
"The mRNA expression of the prototypical Nrf2 target gene Nqo1 was induced in the liver of embryos with maternal calorie restriction almost two-fold. The expression of two glutathione transferases, Gsta1 and Gsta4, was also induced almost eight-fold and two-fold, respectively... In Nrf2 knockout embryos, the induction of Nqo1 and Gsta4 gene expression was not significantly induced after calorie restriction, indicating an Nrf2-dependent effect... we showed that maternal calorie restriction by 50% induced the transcription of antioxidant and cytoprotective genes in fetal murine liver in an at least partially Nrf2-independent manner. In this context, the induction of the prototypical Nrf2 target gene Nqo1 suggests that Nrf2 signaling pathway was activated."
