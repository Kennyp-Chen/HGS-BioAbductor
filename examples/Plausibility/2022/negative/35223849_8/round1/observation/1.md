Would we see some of the observations if the hypothesis was true: Under the hypothesis, chronic alcohol exposure causing impaired autophagy and p62 accumulation could maintain Nrf2 activation, which might contribute to both initially protective antioxidant responses and later anabolic shifts that could favor lipogenesis and proliferation. Thus, while some aspects (p62 accumulation, Nrf2 activation) are common threads, this proposed causal chain does not uniquely account for the observed outcomes.

In that sense, the hypothesis does not offer a clearly novel causal explanation over these known mechanisms.

The hypothesis links chronic alcohol exposure to impaired autophagy, p62 accumulation, and sustained Nrf2 activation as a driver for anabolic reprogramming and progression to hepatocellular carcinoma.
