1. Hypothesis Summary:
The hypothesis posits that in non-alcoholic fatty liver disease (NAFLD), impaired autophagy leads to the accumulation of the autophagy adaptor protein p62. This buildup of p62 results in sustained activation of the transcription factor Nrf2. The persistent Nrf2 activation is then proposed to reprogram hepatocyte metabolism in a way that promotes a tumor-friendly environment, ultimately driving the progression from NAFLD to hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC).
