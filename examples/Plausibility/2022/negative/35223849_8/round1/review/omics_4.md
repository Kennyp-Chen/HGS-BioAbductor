1. Hypothesis Summary  
──────────────────────────────
The hypothesis posits that by precisely modulating the phosphorylation status of p62—via controlled mTOR/ULK1 signaling—it is possible to fine‑tune the duration and amplitude of Keap1 degradation and subsequent Nrf2 activation in liver cells. This finely tuned response is envisioned to provide robust protection against oxidative stress and injury (as observed in conditions like NAFLD or viral hepatitis), while simultaneously avoiding the potential risk of chronic Nrf2 overactivation, which could predispose cells to hepatocellular carcinoma.
