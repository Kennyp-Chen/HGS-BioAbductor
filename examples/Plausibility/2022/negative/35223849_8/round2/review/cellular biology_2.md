1. Hypothesis Summary

The hypothesis proposes that a nanoparticle-based drug delivery system, specifically targeted to liver stellate cells, can pharmacologically enhance p62 phosphorylation. This enhancement is anticipated to promote the autophagic degradation of Keap1, which in turn leads to a controlled activation of the transcription factor Nrf2. By activating Nrf2 in a localized and carefully monitored manner—using integrated omics and imaging methods—the strategy aims to upregulate antioxidant defenses, reduce oxidative stress, and ultimately suppress the activation of hepatic stellate cells, thereby mitigating liver fibrosis. A key safety feature is the confined activation of Nrf2 within an optimized therapeutic window, designed to avoid the oncogenic risks associated with chronic, systemic Nrf2 activation.
