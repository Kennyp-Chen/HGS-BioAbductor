1. Hypothesis Summary  
The hypothesis proposes a dual-intervention strategy to recalibrate the p62/Keap1/Nrf2 axis in liver diseases. It suggests that by combining a targeted, intermittent induction of selective autophagy—achieved via nanoparticle-mediated delivery of autophagy inducers (or optogenetic regulators)—with controlled pharmacological activation of Nrf2 using real-time, biomarker-adjusted dosing, one can periodically clear p62 accumulation. This “reset” of Nrf2 activity to an intermittent, protective pattern is intended to mitigate oxidative injury and prevent the metabolic alterations that lead to fibrosis and tumorigenesis. The strategy leverages advanced drug delivery systems, time-resolved proteomics, and biosensor feedback mechanisms to fine-tune therapy dynamically.
