1. Hypothesis Summary

The hypothesis proposes that a nanoparticle-based, liver stellate cell-targeted drug delivery system can be used to enhance phosphorylation of p62 selectively in hepatic stellate cells. This phosphorylation is expected to trigger autophagic degradation of Keap1, thus releasing and activating Nrf2 in a controlled fashion. Ultimately, this approach is proposed to mitigate liver fibrosis while avoiding the oncogenic risks associated with systemic, chronic activation of Nrf2.
