This upregulation results in iron accumulation, which then supports epithelial-to-mesenchymal transition (EMT) and the maintenance of cancer stem cells. Consequently, the hypothesis suggests that a combined therapeutic strategy—i.e., inhibiting IL-6/STAT3 signaling while simultaneously modulating iron homeostasis—could disrupt an oncogenic feed-forward loop that increases tumor aggressiveness and diminishes treatment responses.
