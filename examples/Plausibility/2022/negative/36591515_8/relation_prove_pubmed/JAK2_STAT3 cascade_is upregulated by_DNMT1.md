## "LncRNA TINCR favors tumorigenesis via STAT3-TINCR-EGFR-feedback loop by recruiting DNMT1 and acting as a competing endogenous RNA in human breast cancer." PMID: 33446634
"Moreover, TINCR stimulates JAK2–STAT3 signaling downstream from EGFR, and STAT3 reciprocally enhances the transcriptional expression of TINCR. ... Mechanistically, TINCR recruits DNMT1 to the miR-503-5p locus promoter, which increases the methylation and suppresses the transcriptional expression of miR-503-5p." However, the full text shows that STAT3 (as part of the JAK2/STAT3 cascade) upregulates TINCR, and TINCR then recruits DNMT1 for methylation activity; there is no evidence that the JAK2/STAT3 cascade directly upregulates DNMT1 expression or activity—rather, DNMT1 recruitment is an effect of TINCR function that is itself regulated by STAT3.

# Conclusion
There is no direct or semantically implied evidence that the JAK2/STAT3 cascade upregulates DNMT1. Instead, the sequence is: JAK2/STAT3 upregulates TINCR, which in turn recruits DNMT1.
