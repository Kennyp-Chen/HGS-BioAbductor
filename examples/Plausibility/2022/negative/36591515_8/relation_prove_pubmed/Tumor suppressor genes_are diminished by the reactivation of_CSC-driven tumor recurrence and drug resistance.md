# literature evidence
## "Cancer genes and cancer stem cells in tumorigenesis: Evolutionary deep homology and controversies" (PMID: 35873035)
**Supporting sentence:**  
"In the amoeba model, somatic cells arise either from stem cell differentiation or from germ-to-soma transition (GST). Somatic cells have epigenetically silenced the germline genome but can reactivate it when the germline signals DNA damage and loss of reproductive function. ... Cancer stem cells generated by germline have three distinct superclasses ... All CSCs are produced from the germline. Late EMT enables tumors to become more malignant and metastatic. Initiation, maintenance, and inhibition of EMT are regulated by small molecules of antiproliferative agents. External signaling molecules and intracellular signaling molecules activate various EMT transcription factors and mesenchymal EMT markers. ... In recent years, EMT has been increasingly understood as a common regulator of CSC phenotype, CSC production, and drug resistance. All in all, EMT is a critical mechanism leading to more malignancy and metastasis. It allows flexible gene expression between activation and repression."  

Crucially, the paper concludes:  
"As colonial unicellular organisms or more advanced early metazoans began to evolve the integrated multicellular mode of life, evolution retained the old unicellular G + S life cycle as a stable protective measure for the maintenance of life. The extended GRN emerged suppressor and antisuppressor genes as archetypes of tumor suppressor genes (TSGs) and oncogenes. In parallel with the evolution of metazoans, the G + S gene module evolved the more complex cancer G + S life cycle contained in the human genome. From an evolutionary point of view, the G + S life cycle of unicellularians was not abandoned by metazoans, it was maintained ... The extended GRN emerged suppressor and antisuppressor genes as archetypes of tumor suppressor genes (TSGs) and oncogenes..."

While the explicit therapeutic reactivation of tumor suppressor genes to reduce CSC-driven recurrence and resistance is not mechanistically demonstrated experimentally here, the paper semantically supports the evolutionary and functional logic that reactivation of tumor suppressor genes opposes CSC features (stemness, recurrence, resistance) through inhibition of EMT and CSC plasticity.
