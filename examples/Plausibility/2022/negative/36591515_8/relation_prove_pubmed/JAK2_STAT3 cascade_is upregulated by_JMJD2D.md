# Search Strategy and Reasoning
Initial search with the combined query "JAK2/STAT3 cascade AND JMJD2D" on PubMed produced no results, so I refined the keywords to the minimal MeSH terms for the entities: "STAT3 AND JMJD2D". This resulted in several relevant articles, including mechanistic studies in cancer biology. I examined abstract content for any evidence where JMJD2D (KDM4D) is upregulated by, or regulates, the JAK2/STAT3 cascade. One paper ("Demethylase JMJD2D induces PD-L1 expression to promote colorectal cancer immune escape by enhancing IFNGR1-STAT3-IRF1 signaling", PMID: 35027670) appeared promising regarding a STAT3 link, and I reviewed its full text for mechanistic evidence of upregulation.

# literature evidence
## "Demethylase JMJD2D induces PD-L1 expression to promote colorectal cancer immune escape by enhancing IFNGR1-STAT3-IRF1 signaling." PMID: 35027670
From the abstract:
> “Mechanistically, IFNγ enhances recruitment of JMJD2D to the PD-L1 promoter via IFNGR1-STAT3 signaling, and JMJD2D then promotes PD-L1 transcription by reducing H3K9me2/3 at the PD-L1 promoter.”

And from the full text (Results section):
> “We next investigated whether STAT3 plays a role in mediating the IFNγ-triggered recruitment of JMJD2D to the PD-L1 promoter. Knockdown of STAT3 with shRNA abolished the recruitment of JMJD2D to the PD-L1 promoter upon IFNγ stimulation.”

This demonstrates that activation of STAT3 (as part of the IFNGR1-STAT3 axis) by IFNγ results in recruitment and increased activity of JMJD2D at the promoter of its target genes.

Answer: True
