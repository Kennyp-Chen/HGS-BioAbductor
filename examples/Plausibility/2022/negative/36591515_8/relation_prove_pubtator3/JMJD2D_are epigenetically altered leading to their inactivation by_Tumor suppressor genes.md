## "Demethylase-independent function of JMJD2D as a novel antagonist of p53 to promote Liver Cancer initiation and progression" (PMID: 32754284)
"Mechanistically, JMJD2D directly interacted with p53 and inhibited p53 recruitment to the p21 and PUMA promoters in a demethylation activity-independent manner, implicating a demethylase-independent function of JMJD2D as a novel p53 antagonist... JMJD2D can antagonize the tumor suppressor p53 and activate an oncogenic signaling pathway ... to promote liver cancer initiation and progression."

## "Histone Demethylase JMJD2D: A Novel Player in Colorectal and Hepatocellular Cancers" (PMID: 35740507)
"JMJD2D serves as a coactivator of beta-catenin, Gli1/2, HIF1alpha, STAT3, IRF1, TCF4, and NICD or an antagonist of p53 to promote the progression of CRC and HCC... JMJD2D can bind to p53 to block its transcriptional activity, thereby reducing the expression of p21 and PUMA tumor suppressors in HCC."
