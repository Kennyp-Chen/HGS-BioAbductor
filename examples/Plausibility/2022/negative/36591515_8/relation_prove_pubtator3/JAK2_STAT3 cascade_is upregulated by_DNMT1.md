# literature evidence
## "THAP9-AS1 Promotes Tumorigenesis and Reduces ROS Generation through the JAK2/STAT3 Signaling Pathway by Increasing SOCS3 Promoter Methylation in Osteosarcoma" (PMID: 34691358)
"ChIP and RIP assays were performed in MG63 cells. The results of the ChIP assay showed that the methyltransferases DNMT1, DNMT3a, and DNMT3b were enriched in the promoter of SOCS3 (Figure 2(d)). The results of the RIP assay showed that THAP9-AS1 exhibited a higher conjugation rate with DNMT1, DNMT3a, and DNMT3b (Figure 2(e)). ... the western blot and immunofluorescence (IF) assay data showed that THAP9-AS1 activated the JAK2/STAT3 signaling pathway by upregulating p-JAK2 and p-STAT3 and the nuclear translocation of p-STAT3."

From the Results and Discussion:
"Our data expanded the knowledge of THAP9-AS1 as a possible upstream regulator of the SOCS3/JAK2/STAT3 pathway in OS development."
"Silencing of DNMT1, DNMT3a, and DNMT3b restored the mRNA and protein levels of SOCS3 in OS cells. ... The results of ChIP, RIP, MSP, and BSP assays validated that THAP9-AS1 might recruit DNMTs to the promoter of SOCS3 and lead to the hypermethylation status of the SOCS3 promoter region."
"THAP9-AS1 contributed to the nuclear transfer of p-STAT3, and the protein expression of p-JAK2 and p-STAT3 (Tyr705) in OS cells was reversed by SOCS3. These data revealed that THAP9-AS1 activated JAK2/STAT3 signaling by inhibiting SOCS3 in OS."

However, reversing this pathway (the direct upregulation of DNMT1 by the JAK2/STAT3 cascade): The paper supports that DNMT1, via recruitment by THAP9-AS1, influences JAK2/STAT3 activation by SOCS3 silencing. There's no direct evidence in this study that JAK2/STAT3 activity upregulates DNMT1. Instead, the axis is: THAP9-AS1 recruits DNMT1 → SOCS3 methylation → JAK2/STAT3 activation. The data only indicate DNMT1 upregulates JAK2/STAT3 activity, not that the JAK2/STAT3 cascade upregulates DNMT1 expression or function.

Answer: False
