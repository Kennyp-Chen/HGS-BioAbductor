# Search Strategy and Reasoning
I began by breaking the initial query into minimal MeSH-like terms: "JAK2/STAT3 cascade" and "JMJD2D". I recognized that "JMJD2D" is a synonym for the gene KDM4D. Since "JAK2/STAT3 cascade" is a pathway not always tagged explicitly, I used "STAT3" (the key signal transducer in this cascade) combined with "KDM4D". Searching PubTator3 for papers jointly mentioning "STAT3" and "KDM4D" (JMJD2D) yielded relevant results. I scanned the abstracts for evidence that the JAK2/STAT3 cascade can upregulate (induce/increase expression of) JMJD2D.

# literature evidence
## "Histone demethylase JMJD2D protects against enteric bacterial infection via up-regulating colonic IL-17F to induce beta-defensin expression" (PMID: 38905308)
"JMJD2D was significantly up-regulated in colonic epithelial cells of mice in response to Citrobacter rodentium infection... Mechanistically, JMJD2D activated STAT3 signaling by inducing STAT3 phosphorylation and cooperated with STAT3 to induce IL-17F expression by interacting with STAT3 and been recruited to the IL-17F promoter to demethylate H3K9me3."

Additionally, from the abstract:
"JMJD2D was significantly up-regulated in colonic epithelial cells of mice in response to Citrobacter rodentium infection."

However, while JMJD2D upregulates/activates STAT3 signaling, the reverse (STAT3 upregulating JMJD2D) is **not** supported here or in other abstracts reviewed.

# literature evidence
## "Histone Demethylase JMJD2D: A Novel Player in Colorectal and Hepatocellular Cancers" (PMID: 35740507)
"JMJD2D serves as a coactivator of beta-catenin, Gli1/2, HIF1alpha, STAT3, IRF1, TCF4, and NICD or an antagonist of p53 to promote the progression of CRC and HCC."

This again shows JMJD2D acting on STAT3, not the other way around.

# Conclusion

There is no literature evidence that the JAK2/STAT3 cascade upregulates JMJD2D (KDM4D/JMJD2D). Instead, evidence suggests JMJD2D can activate STAT3.
