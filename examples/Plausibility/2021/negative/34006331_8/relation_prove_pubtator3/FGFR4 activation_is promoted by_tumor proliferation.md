# Search Strategy and Reasoning
The minimal keywords used were "FGFR4 activation" and "tumor proliferation". These queries were searched directly, and then refined to "FGFR4 tumor proliferation" to maximize retrieval of abstracts discussing the linkage between FGFR4 (and its activation) and tumor cell proliferation. Reviewing the abstracts, several directly mention the role of FGFR4 activation or overexpression in promoting tumor proliferation, growth, invasion, or aggressiveness, clearly supporting the connection.
