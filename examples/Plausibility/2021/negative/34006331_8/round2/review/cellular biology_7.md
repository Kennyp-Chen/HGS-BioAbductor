1. Hypothesis Summary  
────────────────────────────  
The hypothesis posits that in hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC), overexpression of PCSK9 contributes to tumor immune evasion by two main pathways. First, PCSK9 promotes the degradation of MHC class I molecules, which impairs the tumor cell’s ability to present antigens to the immune system. Second, it alters lipid metabolism in such a way that it creates a protective shield against ferroptosis—a form of regulated cell death. The hypothesis further suggests that inhibiting PCSK9 using modern genomic and proteomic tools will restore MHC class I expression and disrupt the metabolic shield. When this inhibition is combined with PD-1 checkpoint blockade therapy, the reactivation of CD8+ T-cell responses will be enhanced, leading to a more robust antitumor immune effect.
