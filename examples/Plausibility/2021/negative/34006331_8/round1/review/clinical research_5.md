1. Hypothesis Summary

The hypothesis posits that in advanced hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC), therapeutic inhibition of PCSK9 will normalize or restore LDL receptor (LDLR)–mediated T cell receptor (TCR) recycling on T cells.
