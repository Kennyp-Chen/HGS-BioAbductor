 • Nanoparticles (e.g., cRGD-modified dual-functional carriers) are designed to selectively deliver boron to the tumor, thereby enhancing the local radiation dose and minimizing off-target effects.
 • Real-time imaging and immune biomarker feedback guide the optimized timing for administering immune adjuvants or checkpoint inhibitors. This step is intended to overcome local immune suppression by activating dendritic cells and recruiting T cells.
 • Overall, the combination is posited to transform “cold” tumors into “hot” tumors, eliciting both localized and systemic (abscopal) anti-tumor responses.
