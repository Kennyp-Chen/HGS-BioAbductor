# Search Strategy and Reasoning
Initial search used the minimal keyword set: "BNCT AND 'tumor cell DNA' AND double-strand breaks". No results were found, so the query was simplified to "BNCT AND DNA double-strand breaks", which yielded multiple relevant papers. Abstracts were screened for direct evidence linking BNCT neutron irradiation (as a high-LET radiation modality) to the induction of DNA double-strand breaks (DSBs) in tumor cells. The selected literature was then examined for explicit description of this mechanism.
