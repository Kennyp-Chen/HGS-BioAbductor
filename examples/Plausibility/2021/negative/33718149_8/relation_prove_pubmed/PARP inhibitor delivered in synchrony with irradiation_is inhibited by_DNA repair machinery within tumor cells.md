## "PARP inhibition radiosensitizes BRCA1 wildtype and mutated breast cancer to proton therapy." (PMID: 39730675)
From the abstract: "PARP inhibition amplified the cell kill effect to both photons and protons... We found that PARP inhibition increases radiosensitization independent of BRCA1 status for both protons and photons. The combination of protons and PARP inhibition was the most effective in decreasing clonogenic cell survival, increasing DNA damage, and delaying tumor growth."

From the full text: "PARP inhibition can lead to unresolved SSB lesions and bulky adducts at single-strand damage sites because of PARP1 trapping... Both lesion types eventually require BRCA1-mediated HR for the cell to remain viable, which is unavailable in BRCA1-mutant cell lines, leading to cell death. PARP inhibition increased the radiosensitivity to both radiation types and generally reduced the RBE... Notably, however, even though the RBE was modestly reduced or unchanged, combining protons with PARP inhibition was the most effective treatment for cell killing, with the lowest D_10% observed in all cell lines tested owing to the higher LET of protons. This is an important consideration for clinical translation when different radiation modalities are combined with a radiosensitizing agent.”
