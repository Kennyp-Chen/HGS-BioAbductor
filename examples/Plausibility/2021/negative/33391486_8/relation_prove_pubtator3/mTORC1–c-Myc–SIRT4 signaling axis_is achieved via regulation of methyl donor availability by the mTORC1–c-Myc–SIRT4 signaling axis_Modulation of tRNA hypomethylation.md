Upon abstract review:

- Some evidence links c-Myc with regulation of translation machinery and tRNA modification enzymes.
- SIRT4 is recognized as a mitochondrial sirtuin impacting metabolic regulation, but a direct link to tRNA methylation/hypomethylation regulation via the triad axis, and specifically via methyl donor availability, is not explicit in the literature reviewed.

No abstract or full text directly demonstrates that modulation of tRNA hypomethylation "is achieved via regulation of methyl donor availability by the mTORC1–c-Myc–SIRT4 signaling axis." Evidence exists for partial connections (e.g., mTORC1 regulates tRNA modifications, c-Myc can impact tRNA expression/modification, metabolic state controls methyl donor pools), but there is no single work confirming the axis operates as described in the question.
