# Search Strategy and Reasoning
The minimal keyword candidates are "NSUN2", "metabolic stress", "cancer", and "post-translational modifications". The precise relationship to validate is whether NSUN2 post-translational modifications in cancer cells are triggered by metabolic stress. 

I searched for literature using combinations of:
- "NSUN2 metabolic stress"
- "NSUN2 post-translational modification cancer"

Abstracts were reviewed for any direct or implied evidence linking NSUN2 post-translational modifications to a metabolic stress trigger in cancer cells. Some articles discussed NSUN2's role in promoting metabolic/glycolytic pathways in cancer (e.g., via m5C modifications of metabolic enzymes) and others discussed post-translational modifications of NSUN2 (SUMOylation) promoting cancer, but crucially, none explicitly or implicitly connect metabolic stress in cancer cells as a trigger for NSUN2 post-translational modifications.
