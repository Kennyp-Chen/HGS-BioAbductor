1. Observation extraction:
 • Abstract 1 shows that a member of the RNA methyltransferase family (NSUN7) is epigenetically silenced in liver cancer, leading to loss of mRNA methylation (e.g., of CCDC9B) and affecting protein stability and drug sensitivity.

 • Abstract 3 reveals that dietary methionine restriction reprograms metabolic signaling via an mTORC1–c-Myc–SIRT4 axis that alters methylation potential (SAM production) and gene expression to promote HCC progression.
