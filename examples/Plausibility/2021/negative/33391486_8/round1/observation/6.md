Observation extraction:
• tRFs/tsRNAs are generated from mature or precursor tRNAs in response to stress and are dysregulated in numerous cancers.  
• They participate in posttranscriptional regulation, for example through microRNA‐like effects, interference with translation initiation, and even binding to cytochrome c to prevent apoptosis.  
• Their altered expression is used in the diagnostic and prognostic assessment of various cancers, indicating they influence tumor progression.  
• Although tRFs are linked to translation regulation and cellular survival under stress, none of the abstracts directly report that they localize to mitochondria or specifically regulate mitochondrial translation.

Therefore, this does not provide a novel explanation; it is not a missing piece.

Thus, the mitochondrial translation regulation hypothesis does not add a new causal layer here, and it is not a missing piece.
