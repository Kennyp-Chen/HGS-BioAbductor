1. Observation extraction:
 • Abstract 1: HepG2 cells grown in a three‐dimensional “Cell Dome” system form hemispherical aggregates. In these 3D cultures, cells display higher Pi-class Glutathione S-Transferase enzymatic activity, elevated hypoxia inducible factor-1α (HIF-1α) gene expression, and increased tolerance to mitomycin C compared to standard 2D cultures.
 • Abstract 2: When cultured as spheroids, HepG2 cells exhibit a distinct metabolic profile—particularly showing enhanced lipid metabolism (increased total cholesterol and triglyceride levels)—while primary hepatocytes (rPH) have stronger glucose metabolic features. These metabolic and enzyme activity differences help establish in vitro platforms for drug screening.
 • Abstract 3: 3D tumor spheroids made from liver cancer cells show growth patterns that are modulated by cellular densities and interactions with stromal components (e.g., fibroblasts or related conditioned media), suggesting that the 3D configuration better replicates in vivo conditions and influences cell behavior (e.g., proliferation).
