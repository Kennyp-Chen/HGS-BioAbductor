This maturation is expected to be characterized by the restoration of key drug-metabolizing enzyme expression, thereby improving the cell line's relevance and accuracy for drug metabolism and toxicity screening.

The idea of modulating differentiation status in HepG2 cells to enhance their utility for pharmacological studies is not entirely new. However, focusing on the role of DLK1 as a critical downstream effector of abnormal Wnt/β‐catenin signaling in this context offers a more refined insight.
