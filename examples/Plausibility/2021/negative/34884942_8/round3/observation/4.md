Observation 2:

Observation 3:

Yes – if targeted activation of PGC-1α in HepG2 cells boosts mitochondrial biogenesis and SER (smooth endoplasmic reticulum) remodeling, this could contribute to a reorganization of metabolic regulatory networks and activation of hepatocyte-specific transcription factors like HNF4α.
