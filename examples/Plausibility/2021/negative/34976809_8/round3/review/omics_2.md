1. Hypothesis Summary  
──────────────────────────────  
The hypothesis proposes a combinatorial therapeutic strategy for hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) that focuses on disrupting the IL-6/STAT3 signaling axis. The approach consists of two main components:  
• Restoration of SOCS (Suppressor of Cytokine Signaling) activity using either tumor-targeted CRISPR-dCas9 systems or ligand-guided nanoparticle delivery of SOCS mimetic peptides. This aims to re-establish negative regulation of cytokine signaling pathways.  
• Concurrent selective inhibition of STAT3 to directly reduce transcription of genes linked to cell survival, cancer stemness, and immune evasion (via PD-L1 expression).  
Additionally, the hypothesis includes monitoring STAT1 activity as a safeguard against compensatory signaling mechanisms that might emerge, thereby refining the overall strategy. Together, these interventions are suggested to not only weaken tumor cell proliferation and evasion mechanisms but also potentiate the effects of conventional chemo- and immunotherapies in HCC.
