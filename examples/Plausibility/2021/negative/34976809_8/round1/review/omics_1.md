1. Hypothesis Summary  
─────────────────────────────  
The hypothesis posits that in hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC), chronic inflammatory conditions lead to sustained activation of the IL‐6/STAT3 signaling pathway. This persistent signaling is proposed to epigenetically silence SOCS3—a negative regulator of STAT3—thereby creating a self-reinforcing (feed-forward) loop. This loop allegedly reprograms hepatocyte lipid metabolism in a way that favors tumor initiation, progression, and resistance to therapy. Consequently, the hypothesis suggests that a therapeutic strategy simultaneously targeting STAT3 activity and reversing the epigenetic silencing of its negative regulators (like SOCS3) could be effective against HCC.

• Thus, while components of the hypothesis are grounded in established knowledge, the integration of these elements—especially the connection to lipid metabolism change and therapy resistance—is an innovative perspective in the field.
