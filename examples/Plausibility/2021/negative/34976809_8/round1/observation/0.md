Overactive STAT3, activated by cytokines such as IL-6, mediates interactions between tumor and stromal cells and establishes a pro-tumor environment. (Note: Some evidence even suggests STAT3 may have dual roles regarding tumor promotion versus suppression.)
 • Abstract 3: Both STAT3 and STAT1 are activated in HCC cells. In settings of high IL-6 signaling, STAT3 (and in its absence STAT1) regulates target gene expression. High STAT activity is linked with increased PD-L1 expression and a marked immune infiltrate in liver cancer.
