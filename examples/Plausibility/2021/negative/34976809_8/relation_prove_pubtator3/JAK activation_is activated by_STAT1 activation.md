## "Induction of CIITA by IFN-gamma in macrophages involves STAT1 activation by JAK and JNK." (PMID: 34303919)
"In an early phase, during the first 2 h of IFN-gamma treatment, STAT1 is phosphorylated at Tyrosine 701 and Serine 727, residues required for the induction of the transcription factor IRF1. In a later phase, STAT1 phosphorylation and JNK activation are required to trigger CIIta expression... Our findings demonstrate that STAT1 is required in a two-step induction of CIIta, once again highlighting the significance of cross talk between signaling pathways in macrophages."
- This abstract directly supports that JAK activity (as part of the IFN-gamma response) leads to STAT1 phosphorylation and activation.
