# Search Strategy and Reasoning

These keywords focus on Hepatitis B/C Virus infections, persistent liver inflammation, and hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) as the disease context. The abstracts were evaluated for evidence showing that HBV or HCV infection leads to (induces) persistent inflammation, which contributes to HCC. Several abstracts explicitly reference the role of chronic (persistent) inflammation from HBV/HCV contributing to the pathogenesis and development/progression of HCC. I selected the most directly relevant, clearly stating this causal/sequential relationship.
