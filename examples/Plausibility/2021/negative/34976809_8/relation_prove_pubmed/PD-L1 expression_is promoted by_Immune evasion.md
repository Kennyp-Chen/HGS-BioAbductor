# Search Strategy and Reasoning
The initial keyword query was "PD-L1 expression AND Immune evasion" to capture articles explicitly discussing the relationship between PD-L1 expression and immune evasion. I specifically examined the abstracts for any direct or implied semantic link suggesting that PD-L1 expression is promoting immune evasion. Several abstracts describe upregulation of PD-L1 leading to immune escape, particularly in cancer contexts, which tightly matches the queried relationship.
