I used the minimal MeSH term keywords "DNA Damage Response AND immune activation" to search for literature connecting compromised DNA Damage Response (DDR) with intensification or amplification of the immune activation cascade. The abstracts were screened for evidence of a semantically implied amplification: i.e., whether further compromised DDR leads to intensified immune activation. # literature evidence
## "Elucidating DNA Damage-Dependent Immune System Activation." (PMID: 40565309)
"The immune system can be activated by the DDR network through a number of different mechanisms, such as via (a) an increase in the tumor neoantigen burden, (b) the induction of the stimulator of interferon genes pathway, (c) the triggering of immunogenic cell death, (d) an increase in antigen presentation as a result of the augmented expression of the major histocompatibility complex type I molecule, (e) modification of the cytokine milieu in the tumor microenvironment, and (f) altered expression of the programmed cell death ligand-1."

Answer: True
