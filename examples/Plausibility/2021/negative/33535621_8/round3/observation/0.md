3. Causal Analysis (Summary):
 Would we see some of the observations if the hypothesis was true: Yes. However, the role of DDR in modulating the tumor immune microenvironment, including PD-L1 expression, has received attention already in several cancer types. Thus, the dual therapeutic strategy (targeting both TGF‐β and PD-L1) is an interesting combinatorial approach but does not uniquely fill a gap that isn’t already under investigation in the field.

However, the complex and multifactorial nature of the immune response in BTC means that several alternative explanations (e.g., intrinsic tumor immunogenicity and other immune escape mechanisms) could also account for the limited ICI efficacy.
