# literature evidence
 
## "Interferon-gamma induced PD-L1 expression and soluble PD-L1 production in gastric cancer" (PMID: 32782533)
"Interferon (IFN)-gamma treatment of cells resulted in increased cytoplasmic expression of PD-L1 in GC cells in a dose-dependent manner, except for MKN74 cells; however, there was no association between tumor necrosis factor-alpha treatment and enhanced PD-L1 expression. Concordant with these findings, results from flow cytometry analysis demonstrated that membrane-bound PD-L1 expression was also increased following GC cell treatment with IFN-gamma in a dose-dependent manner."

## "Regulation of IFN-gamma-mediated PD-L1 expression by MYC in colorectal cancer with wild-type KRAS and TP53 and its clinical implications" (PMID: 36582540)
"In the tumor microenvironment, interferon gamma (IFN-gamma) secreted by tumor infiltrating lymphocytes can upregulate programmed cell death 1 ligand 1 (PD-L1) expression in many cancers. The present study evaluated the expression of PD-L1 in selected colorectal cancer cell lines with IFN-gamma treatment... experimental data revealed that interferon gamma induced the upregulation of programmed cell death 1 ligand 1 mainly through regulating MYC in wild-type KRAS and TP53 colorectal cancers."

## "Type I IFNs contribute to upregulation of PD-L1 during Chlamydia trachomatis infection" (PMID: 40071913)
"...we discovered that C. trachomatis triggers the secretion of host type I interferons (IFNs) that are necessary and sufficient to upregulate PD-L1 in vitro. Additionally, secretion of type I IFNs is dependent on C. trachomatis development and its type III secretion system. We have also validated that type I IFNs contribute to upregulation of PD-L1 during C. trachomatis infection in vivo using a mouse model of infection."
