This activation leads to an immunogenic tumor microenvironment, evidenced by increased PD-L1 expression and T cell infiltration. Consequently, the hypothesis suggests that combining PARP inhibitors (which target DDR pathways) with immune checkpoint inhibitors could synergistically convert immunologically “cold” tumors into “hot” tumors, thereby enhancing the efficacy of immunotherapy in BTC.
