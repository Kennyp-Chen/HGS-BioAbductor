• A central claim is that TGF‐β signaling—along with cytokines such as IFN‐γ—both enhances PD-L1 expression and recruits immunosuppressive cell populations. The net effect is the development of an “immune cold” tumor microenvironment that undermines the efficacy of immune checkpoint inhibitors.  
• Finally, the hypothesis advocates that combined therapeutic targeting of TGF‐β and PD-L1 (using advanced models and multi-omics analyses) could overcome resistance to immunotherapy in this BTC subset, thereby improving clinical outcomes.
