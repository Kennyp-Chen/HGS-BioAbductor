1. Hypothesis Summary  
─────────────────────────────────────────────  
The hypothesis posits that in hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) under hypoxic conditions, inhibiting the enzyme ENTPD2 can diminish the immunosuppressive functions mediated by myeloid-derived suppressor cells (MDSCs). This reduction in immunosuppression is proposed to restore the function of effector immune cells, which in turn enhances the therapeutic effectiveness of immune checkpoint inhibitors (ICIs) in advanced HCC.
