1. Hypothesis Summary  
─────────────────────────────  
The hypothesis posits that in hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC), the hypoxic tumor microenvironment activates the ENTPD2 pathway, which in turn promotes the accumulation or functional activity of myeloid-derived suppressor cells (MDSCs). This immunosuppressive milieu hampers cytotoxic T-lymphocyte (CTL) responses. The proposed strategy is to combine targeting the hypoxia-driven ENTPD2 pathway with PD-1 checkpoint blockade, thereby reducing MDSC-mediated immunosuppression and reinvigorating CTLs, with the ultimate goal of enhancing antitumor efficacy.
