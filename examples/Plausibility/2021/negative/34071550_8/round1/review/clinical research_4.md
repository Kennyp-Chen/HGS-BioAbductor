1. Hypothesis Summary

Emerging evidence in tumor immunology has focused on numerous enzymes linked to the immunosuppressive cascade; however, the specific role of ENTPD2 in modulating MDSCs—particularly within hypoxic regions of HCC—has not been as thoroughly explored.
