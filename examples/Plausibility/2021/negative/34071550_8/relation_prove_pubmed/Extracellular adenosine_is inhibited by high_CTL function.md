# literature evidence
## "Adenosine A2a Receptor Antagonism Restores Additive Cytotoxicity by Cytotoxic T Cells in Metabolically Perturbed Tumors" (PMID: 36162129)
"Extracellular adenosine is an immunosuppressive metabolite produced within the tumor microenvironment. Here, by applying single-cell reporter strategies in 3D collagen cocultures in vitro and progressing tumors in vivo, we show that adenosine weakens one-to-one pairing of activated effector CTLs with target cells, thereby dampening serial cytotoxic hit delivery and cumulative death induction. Adenosine also severely compromised CTL effector restimulation and expansion."

Additionally (from the full text): 
"Low micromolar concentrations of adenosine, like those observed in tumors in vivo, reduced CTL-mediated tumor cell killing across a wide range of CTL densities and by up to 65%... Thus, adenosine labilizes CTL–target cell interactions, dampens calcium signaling and degranulation, and decreases CTL killing efficacy."

Answer: True
