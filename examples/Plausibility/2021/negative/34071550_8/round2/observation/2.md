1. Observation extraction:
 • Observation 1: HCC is characterized by intratumoral hypoxia with high HIF-1α expression. In a preclinical model, a low‐molecular‐weight HIF inhibitor (32‑134D) changed gene expression affecting angiogenesis, metabolism, and immune responses – notably decreasing immunosuppressive cells such as tumor‐associated macrophages and myeloid‐derived suppressor cells (MDSCs) while increasing CD8+ T cells and natural killer cells.
 • Observation 2: Exosomal non‐coding RNAs in HCC are reported to mediate crosstalk between tumor cells and various immunosuppressive cells in the tumor microenvironment (TME), including cells responsible for maintaining an immune‑suppressive niche.
 • Observation 3: The immunosuppressive TME in HCC – involving cells such as MDSCs, TAMs, TANs, and CAFs – plays a key role in promoting tumor progression and in modulating the efficacy of immune checkpoint blockade therapies.
