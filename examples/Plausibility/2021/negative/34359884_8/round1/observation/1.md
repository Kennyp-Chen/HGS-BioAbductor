1. Observation extraction:
 • HCC exhibits a high dependence on glycolysis for energy production, especially under hypoxic conditions.
 • HCC tumors characteristically upregulate glycolytic enzymes and transporters (e.g., GLUT1), a feature linked with metabolic reprogramming.

 • In certain preclinical models, inhibition of HIF‐mediated signaling changes the tumor immune milieu—increasing CD8⁺ T cells and reducing immunosuppressive cells—improving responses to immune checkpoint blockade.
 • Metabolic reprogramming in HCC is implicated in therapeutic resistance.
