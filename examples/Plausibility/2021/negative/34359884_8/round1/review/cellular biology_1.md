1. Hypothesis Summary

The hypothesis proposes that blocking the lactate receptor GPR81 can disrupt a lactate-driven pathway that upregulates PD-L1 expression. In a hypoxic hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) setting—where lactate levels are elevated—this inhibition is predicted to prevent PD-L1–mediated immunosuppression. When the GPR81 inhibitor is combined with immune checkpoint inhibitors, it is expected to restore the function of CD8⁺ T cells, thus overcoming tumor drug resistance.
