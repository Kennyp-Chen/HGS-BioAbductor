# Search Strategy and Reasoning
I searched PubMed using the minimal keywords "HIF AND glycolysis" to capture literature about the potential for HIF activation to drive or enhance glycolysis, regardless of context or disease. The abstracts were reviewed for semantic evidence linking HIF (particularly HIF-1α, the most studied isoform) activation/function to increased/enhanced glycolysis. Among the results, several abstracts described HIF-1α as a transcription factor that activates genes related to glycolysis, and at least one (PMID: 40543996) provides direct evidence that HIF-1α stabilization leads to increased expression of glycolytic proteins. Although I could not access the full text for piecemeal mechanistic details, the abstract contains a direct and unambiguous connection.
