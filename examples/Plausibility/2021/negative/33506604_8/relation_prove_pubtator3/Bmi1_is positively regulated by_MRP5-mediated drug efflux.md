# Search Strategy and Reasoning
I searched PubMed using the following minimal MeSH terms: "Bmi1 MRP5", "Bmi1 ABCC5", and "Bmi1 drug efflux". ABCC5 is the official gene name of MRP5, which underlies MRP5-mediated drug efflux. Since "MRP5-mediated drug efflux" is specifically tied to ABCC5 expression/function, connecting Bmi1 to ABCC5 would serve as direct evidence. I systematically reviewed abstracts returned by all these queries for any indication that Bmi1 positively regulates (directly or indirectly) ABCC5/MRP5-mediated drug efflux.
