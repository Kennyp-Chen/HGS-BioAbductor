1. Hypothesis Summary  
──────────────────────────────
The hypothesis proposes that the sumoylation of hnRNPA2B1—a post‐translational modification that affects protein function—is crucial for the loading of oncogenic miRNAs (for example, miR-155) into exosomes. Therefore, inhibiting the sumoylation of hnRNPA2B1 would disrupt the loading of these miRNAs into exosomes, ultimately reducing EMT induction and reversing chemoresistance.
