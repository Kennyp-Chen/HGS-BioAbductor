1. Observation extraction:
 • ALBI grade, calculated from albumin and bilirubin levels, is widely used as an objective biomarker of liver function and prognostic stratification in HCC patients.
 • A lower (better) ALBI grade correlates with improved survival, less tumor relapse, and fewer post-procedure complications across both curative and palliative therapies.
 • ALBI grade has been shown to outperform the traditionally used Child-Pugh score in certain settings, supporting its clinical utility in treatment decision‐making.
 • In systemic and locoregional therapies, maintaining hepatic reserve is critical for treatment tolerance and efficacy.
