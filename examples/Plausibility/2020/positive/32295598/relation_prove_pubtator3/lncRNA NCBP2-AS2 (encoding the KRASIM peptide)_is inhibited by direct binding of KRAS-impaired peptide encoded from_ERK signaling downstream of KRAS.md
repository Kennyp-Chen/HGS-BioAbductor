## "Current understanding of functional peptides encoded by lncRNA in cancer" (PMID: 39030557)
"Overexpression of KRASIM can reduce the level of proto-oncogene KRAS protein, which then inhibits the ERK signaling pathway in HCC (hepatocellular carcinoma) cells causing a reduction in the growth and proliferation of HCC cells. In the cytoplasm of human HCC cells, KRASIM interacts and colocalizes with KRAS protein."
