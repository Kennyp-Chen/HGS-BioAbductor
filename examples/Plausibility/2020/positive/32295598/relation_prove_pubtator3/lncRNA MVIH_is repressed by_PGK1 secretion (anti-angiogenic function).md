# Search Strategy and Reasoning
Keywords chosen: "lncRNA MVIH PGK1". The logic was to find any direct literature relating lncRNA MVIH (Minimal Vocabulary: MVIH, MeSH Term: long noncoding RNA) and PGK1 secretion (MeSH Term: Phosphoglycerate Kinase 1). The focus was on whether literature supports the claim that lncRNA MVIH represses (inhibits) the anti-angiogenic function of PGK1 secretion. Search was limited to abstracts first; upon finding promising abstracts, I attempted to confirm via full text, prioritizing the most relevant abstracts that directly discuss repression of PGK1 secretion by MVIH. PMID: 32295598 was excluded from consideration as instructed.
