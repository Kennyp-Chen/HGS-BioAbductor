# Search Strategy and Reasoning
I used the minimal MeSH keywords "HBx" and "lncRNA Dreh" to query PubTator3 for any literature that discusses their relationship. Initial search results showed several abstracts that referenced a possible downregulation mechanism. To ensure the reliability of the evidence and that it was not based solely on reviews, I extracted the most directly relevant and recent PMIDs (excluding review-only results if possible), and attempted to obtain their full texts for the most rigorous support.

# literature evidence

"In contrast to HUR1, the lncRNA DREH (Downregulated expression by HBx) is repressed by HBx and acts as a tumor suppressor in the development of HBV-related HCC."

## "Mechanism of HBx carcinogenesis interaction with non-coding RNA in hepatocellular carcinoma" (PMID: 37746253)
"HBx primarily achieves the downregulation of lncRNA Dreh by suppressing the expression of Dreh."

## "The expression and function of long noncoding RNAs in hepatocellular carcinoma" (PMID: 38125766)
