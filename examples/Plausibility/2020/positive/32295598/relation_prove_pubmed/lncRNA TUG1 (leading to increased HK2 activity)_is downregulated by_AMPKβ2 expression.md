## "Taurine up-regulated gene 1 functions as a master regulator to coordinate glycolysis and metastasis in hepatocellular carcinoma." (PMID: 28802060)
Exact sentence from the abstract:
"Knockdown of taurine up-regulated gene 1 (TUG1) induces marked inhibition of cell migration, invasion, and glycolysis through suppression of microRNA (miR)-455-3p. MiR-455-3p, which is transcriptionally repressed by p21, directly targets the 3' untranslated region of adenosine monophosphate-activated protein kinase subunit beta 2 (AMPKβ2). The TUG1/miR-455-3p/AMPKβ2 axis regulates cell growth, metastasis, and glycolysis through regulation of hexokinase 2 (HK2)."

From the Results section (full text):
"The silencing of TUG1 significantly increased miR-455-3p, which led to a decrease in AMPKβ2 protein levels via miR-455-3p targeting."  
And, importantly:  
"Depletion of TUG1 led to downregulation of HK2 activity via an axis implicating AMPKβ2..."

Interpretation: Knockdown of TUG1 increases miR-455-3p, which reduces AMPKβ2. However, the specific question asks whether TUG1 (by leading to HK2 activity) **downregulates** AMPKβ2 expression. The literature demonstrates that TUG1 supports AMPKβ2 expression (i.e., TUG1 knockdown **decreases** AMPKβ2). Therefore, TUG1 normally acts to upregulate or maintain AMPKβ2, not downregulate it.
