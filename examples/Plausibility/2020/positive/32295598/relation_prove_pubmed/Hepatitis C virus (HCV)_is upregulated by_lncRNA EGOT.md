# Search Strategy and Reasoning
The minimal keyword set is: "Hepatitis C virus AND lncRNA EGOT". 
I searched for papers connecting these terms. I found three relevant articles. I reviewed their abstracts and selected those that specifically assessed whether lncRNA EGOT is upregulated by HCV. PMID: 32295598 was explicitly excluded. Among the retrieved articles, PMID: 27283940 provides a direct statement in the abstract and is also accessible in full text (although the abstract alone is highly specific). It discusses the induction (i.e., upregulation) of lncRNA EGOT by HCV infection both in cell lines and in the liver of patients. This satisfies the query requirement of literature support for lncRNA EGOT being upregulated by HCV.

# literature evidence
## "Long noncoding RNA EGOT negatively affects the antiviral response and favors HCV replication." (PMID: 27283940)
"EGOT is increased in the liver of patients infected with HCV and after infection with influenza or Semliki Forest virus (SFV). ... Our results suggest that EGOT is a lncRNA induced after infection that increases viral replication by antagonizing the antiviral response."

Answer: True
