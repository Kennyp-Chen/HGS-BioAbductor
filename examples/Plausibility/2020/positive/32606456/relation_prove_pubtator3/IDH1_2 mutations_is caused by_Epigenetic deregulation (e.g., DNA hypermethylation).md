# Search Strategy and Reasoning
I used the core MeSH concepts "IDH1", "IDH2", and "DNA hypermethylation" because "IDH1/2 mutations" are most specifically represented by these gene symbols, and "epigenetic deregulation" is best narrowed to "DNA hypermethylation" for search sensitivity without introducing unrelated material. Several abstracts directly examined the molecular consequences of IDH1/2 mutations, focusing on whether they cause DNA hypermethylation or epigenetic deregulation. I avoided the excluded PMID: 32606456.
