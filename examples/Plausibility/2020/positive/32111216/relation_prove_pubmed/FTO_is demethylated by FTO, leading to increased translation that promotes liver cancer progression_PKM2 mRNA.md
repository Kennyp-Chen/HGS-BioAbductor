# Search Strategy and Reasoning
I identified the minimal search keywords as "FTO" (a demethylase) and "PKM2 mRNA" (the mRNA for pyruvate kinase M2). I then searched for literature explicitly linking FTO with the demethylation of PKM2 mRNA and subsequent effects on translation and hepatocellular (liver) cancer. Abstracts were prioritized for direct or semantic descriptions of such a pathway, focusing on m6A demethylation, translation upregulation, and cancer progression. Review articles, mechanistic studies, and full sentences from abstracts were carefully checked to exclude the prohibited PMID and confirm a solid connection relevant to liver cancer.

# literature evidence
## "LncRNA FTO-IT1 promotes glycolysis and progression of hepatocellular carcinoma through modulating FTO-mediated N6-methyladenosine modification on GLUT1 and PKM2" (PMID: 37840133)
"Mechanistically, FTO-IT1 induced stabilization of FTO mRNA by recruiting ILF2/ILF3 protein complex to 3'UTR of FTO mRNA. As a demethylase for N6-methyladenosine (m6A), FTO decreased m6A modification on mRNAs of glycolysis associated genes including GLUT1, PKM2, and c-Myc which alleviated the YTH N6-methyladenosine RNA binding protein 2 (YTHDF2)-mediated mRNA degradation. Therefore, the upregulated expression of FTO-IT1 leaded to overexpression of GLUT1, PKM2, and c-Myc by which enhanced glycolysis of HCC."

## "Functions of N6-methyladenosine in cancer metabolism: from mechanism to targeted therapy" (PMID: 37055798)
"Mechanistically, FTO induces the demethylation of PKM2 mRNA to fuel translation, ultimately resulting in HCC."

## "Intertwined regulation between RNA m6A modification and cancer metabolism" (PMID: 37192910)
"As a dependency factor in hepatocellular carcinoma, FTO triggers the demethylation of pyruvate kinase M2 (PKM2) mRNA, resulting in enhanced mRNA translation."

## "The Potential Role of N6-Methyladenosine (m6A) Demethylase Fat Mass and Obesity-Associated Gene (FTO) in Human Cancers" (PMID: 33364780)
"Overexpressed FTO in the HCC tissues and cells modulated cancer cell proliferation, cell cycle and in vivo tumor growth, mechanically through triggering the demethylation of pyruvate kinase (PKM2) mRNA and accelerating the translation."
