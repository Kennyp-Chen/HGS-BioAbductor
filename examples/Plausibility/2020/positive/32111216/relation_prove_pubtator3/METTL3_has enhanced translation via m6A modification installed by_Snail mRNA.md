## "RNA m6A methyltransferase METTL3 promotes colorectal cancer cell proliferation and invasion by regulating Snail expression" (PMID: 34457066)
Mechanistically, the present study demonstrated that METTL3 methylates Snail mRNA, thus stabilizing it to promote CRC malignancy.

Full text further confirmation:
"Using m6A-RIP-qPCR and Act-D assays, the present study demonstrated that METTL3-mediated m6A enrichment enhances the stability of Snail mRNA during RNA processing... METTL3 depletion can repress Snail in an m6A-dependent manner at the RNA level, indicating that targeting Snail mRNA m6A sites to induce the suppression of expression may be a novel method to prevent CRC progression."
