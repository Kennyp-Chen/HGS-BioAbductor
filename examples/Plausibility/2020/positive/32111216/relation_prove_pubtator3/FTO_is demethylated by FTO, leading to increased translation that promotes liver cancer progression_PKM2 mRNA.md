## "LncRNA FTO-IT1 promotes glycolysis and progression of hepatocellular carcinoma through modulating FTO-mediated N6-methyladenosine modification on GLUT1 and PKM2" (PMID: 37840133)
"As a demethylase for N6-methyladenosine (m6A), FTO decreased m6A modification on mRNAs of glycolysis associated genes including GLUT1, PKM2, and c-Myc which alleviated the YTH N6-methyladenosine RNA binding protein 2 (YTHDF2)-mediated mRNA degradation. Therefore, the upregulated expression of FTO-IT1 leaded to overexpression of GLUT1, PKM2, and c-Myc by which enhanced glycolysis of HCC."
