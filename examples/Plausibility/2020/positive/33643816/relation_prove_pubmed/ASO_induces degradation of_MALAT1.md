# Reasoning
Both abstracts provide direct semantic evidence that ASOs targeting MALAT1 substantially reduce its expression—which, for lncRNAs like MALAT1 and in the context of ASO mechanisms, is widely understood as resulting from RNase H-dependent degradation of the RNA. Thus, this offers credible literature evidence supporting the claim that ASO induces degradation of MALAT1.
