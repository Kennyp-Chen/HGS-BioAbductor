# Reasoning summary
In all referenced studies, siRNA specifically designed to target lncRNA CASC9 was transfected into cells, resulting in a decrease of CASC9 RNA levels as measured by RT-qPCR. While the studies often focus on the phenotypic outcomes (like changes in proliferation), the use of siRNAs—with the observed decrease of CASC9 transcripts—demonstrates that siRNA induces degradation (through the canonical RNA interference pathway resulting in endonucleolytic cleavage and reduced abundance of the target lncRNA transcript). This applies both to CASC9 in cancer cell lines and in multiple studies across different cancer models.
