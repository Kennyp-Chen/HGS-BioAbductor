# Search Strategy and Reasoning
I searched PubTator using the minimal keywords: "H19 E-cadherin" because E-cadherin is encoded by CDH1, and H19 is well-annotated as a lncRNA. The key semantic criterion for "downregulates" is to find literature where H19 overexpression leads to reduced E-cadherin/CDH1 levels or where knocking down H19 increases E-cadherin expression. I focused first on abstracts mentioning both genes in direct regulatory context, and then verified support in the full text of the top-ranked papers.

# literature evidence
