# Search Strategy and Reasoning
I queried PubMed for connections between "PFKFB3" and "PFK1", including their full names ("6-phosphofructo-2-kinase" and "6-phosphofructo-1-kinase"), and then directly between "fructose-2,6-bisphosphate" (the product generated by PFKFB3) and "PFK1", as F-2,6-BP is known to allosterically activate PFK1. The results consistently describe that PFKFB3 produces fructose-2,6-bisphosphate, which in turn allosterically activates PFK1. Abstracts across multiple recent papers (excluding PMID: 32631382) explicitly support this indirect allosteric activation mechanism as the literature consensus.
