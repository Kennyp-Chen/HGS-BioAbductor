## "Hypoxia-induced lncRNA-AC020978 promotes proliferation and glycolytic metabolism of non-small cell lung cancer by regulating PKM2/HIF-1alpha axis", PMID: 32308748
"Moreover, AC020978 was an upregulated lncRNA under glucose starvation as well as hypoxia conditions, and directly transactivated by HIF-1alpha. Mechanistic investigations... uncovered that AC020978 could promote the nuclear translocation of PKM2 and regulate PKM2-enhanced HIF-1alpha transcription activity."

"In addition to its central role as a pyruvate enzyme at cytoplasm, PKM2 may also translocate into the nucleus to function as a protein kinase and interact with many tumor-associated genes to accelerate carcinogenesis, including HIF-1alpha... We observed that AC020978 could increase both total and nuclear PKM2, and enhance the PKM2/HIF-1alpha interaction, consequently modulate gene transcription in the HIF-1alpha system."
