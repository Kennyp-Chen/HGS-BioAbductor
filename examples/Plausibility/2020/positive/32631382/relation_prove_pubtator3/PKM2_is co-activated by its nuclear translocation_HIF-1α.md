# literature evidence

## "The HIF-1alpha antisense long non-coding RNA drives a positive feedback loop of HIF-1alpha mediated transactivation and glycolysis" (PMID: 33637716)
**Supporting sentences from full text:**
1. "It has been shown that pyruvate kinase isozymes M2 (PKM2) acts as the essential co-activator to stimulate HIF-1 transactivation in tumor cells. Particularly under hypoxic condition, PHD3 binds to PKM2 to induce its prolyl hydroxylation in the cytoplasm. Then, the PKM2/PHD3 complex is transported into the nucleus and assists recruitment of HIF-1 as well as p300 to form a transcriptional complex at the hypoxia response elements (HRE)."
2. "We revealed that HIFAL serves as a scaffold to recruit PHD3 to PKM2 and induces the nuclear translocation of PKM2/PHD3 complex to activate HIF-1 transcription in human breast cancer. Together, these findings highlighted that the HIFAL was essential for PKM2 to drive HIF-1alpha mediated transcription in cancer development."

## "Dihydrotanshinone I preconditions myocardium against ischemic injury via PKM2 glutathionylation sensitive to ROS" (PMID: 36815040)
**Supporting sentence from abstract:**
"In the nucleus, PKM2 served as a co-factor to promote HIF-1alpha-dependent gene induction, contributing to adaptive responses."

# Conclusion
These full-text mechanistic studies provide direct evidence that PKM2 and HIF-1α can be co-activated through a process in which PKM2's nuclear translocation enables it to function as a co-activator for HIF-1α-mediated transcription. This phenomenon occurs in the presence of other factors (PHD3, or via ROS/glutathionylation), and both proteins functionally cooperate inside the nucleus, supporting the specific relationship described.
