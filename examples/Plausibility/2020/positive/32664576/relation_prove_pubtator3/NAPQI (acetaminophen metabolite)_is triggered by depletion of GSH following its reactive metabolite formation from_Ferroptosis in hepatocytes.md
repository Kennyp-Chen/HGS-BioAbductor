## "Maternal Gut Inflammation Aggravates Acute Liver Failure Through Facilitating Ferroptosis via Altering Gut Microbial Metabolism in Offspring" (PMID: 39808540)
**Supporting sentence from full text:**  
"In general, excessive APAP is metabolized by cytochrome p450 2E1 (CYP2E1) into toxic N-acetyl-p-benzoquinone imine (NAPQI), which binds to functional proteins to form APAP-protein adducts, resulting in excessive oxidative stress, ferroptosis and ALF. Notably, NAPQI can be rapidly detoxified by glutathione (GSH), an antioxidant that reduces reactive oxygen species (ROS) via glutathione peroxidase 4 (GPX4), one of the central regulators of ferroptosis. Hence, enhancing the GSH/GPX4 axis may serve as a potential strategy for intervention in APAP-induced ferroptosis and ALF."
