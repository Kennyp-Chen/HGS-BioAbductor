## "Cyclooxygenase-2-Prostaglandin E2 pathway: A key player in tumor-associated immune cells" (PMID: 36776289)
**Supporting sentence from full text:**  
"COX-2-PGE2 Pathway in Tumor Immunology: The COX-2-PGE2 pathway induces tumor immune evasion by regulating myeloid-derived suppressor cells (MDSCs)... MDSCs mediate immunosuppression by producing arginase and inducible nitric oxide synthase, and TGF-beta, IL-10 and COX-2. These substances directly or indirectly enhance Treg activity, inhibit cytotoxic activity of NK cells, and promote the polarization of macrophages toward M2-like phenotype."  
And, crucially:  
"Taken together, the COX-2-PGE2 signaling pathway promotes tumor immune evasion by inducing the accumulation of MDSCs cells, which in turn promotes tumor growth and development."
