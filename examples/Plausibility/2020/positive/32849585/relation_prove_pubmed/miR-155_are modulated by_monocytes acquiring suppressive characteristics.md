## "TLR or NOD receptor signaling skews monocyte fate decision via distinct mechanisms driven by mTOR and miR-155."
PMID: 34686603

"Mechanistically, we found that pathogen sensing through toll-like receptor (TLR) ligands increases mo-mac differentiation via mTORC1. By contrast, nucleotide-binding oligomerization domain (NOD) ligands favor mo-DC through the induction of TNF-alpha secretion and miR-155 expression. We confirmed these results in vivo, in mouse skin and by analyzing transcriptomic data from human individuals."
