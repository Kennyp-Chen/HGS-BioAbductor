# literature evidence
## "Retinoic Acid Induces an IFN-Driven Inflammatory Tumour Microenvironment, Sensitizing to Immune Checkpoint Therapy" (PMID: 35402250)
Although it is known that tretinoin preferentially depletes myeloid derived suppressor cells in blood, little is known about the effects of tretinoin on the tumour microenvironment, hampering the rational design of clinical trials using tretinoin in combination with ICT.

## "Modulation of Immunosuppression by Oligonucleotide-Based Molecules and Small Molecules Targeting Myeloid-Derived Suppressor Cells" (PMID: 31431006)
This review summarizes MDSC-targeting oligonucleotides and small molecules, but does not provide direct evidence that ATRA induces the differentiation of MDSCs into mature, non-suppressive myeloid cells.

## "Endogenous and combination retinoids are active in myelomonocytic leukemias" (PMID: 33241677)
Pharmacologic retinoid responses could be optimized by concurrent targeting RXR ligands (e.g. bexarotene) and RARA ligands (e.g. all-trans retinoic acid, ATRA), which induced either leukemic maturation or apoptosis depending on cell culture conditions.
