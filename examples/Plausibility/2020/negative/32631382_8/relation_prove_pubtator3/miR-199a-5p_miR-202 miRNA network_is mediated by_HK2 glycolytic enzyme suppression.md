# Search Strategy and Reasoning
Minimal MeSH Term queries used: 
- "miR-199a-5p | HK2" and "miR-202 | HK2", focusing on direct gene/protein terms since "miRNA network" and detailed mechanism descriptors are not MeSH terms. The query was split to individually examine the literature evidence for "miR-199a-5p" and "miR-202" regulating or mediating suppression of "HK2" (Hexokinase 2), the critical glycolytic enzyme.

Rationale:
- The investigation focused on whether there are papers specifically showing the miRNAs miR-199a-5p and/or miR-202 directly or indirectly lead to HK2 suppression (which would indicate mediation).
- A network-level interaction is less likely to be stated explicitly in abstracts, but if both components are linked functionally (one regulates or targets the other resulting in enzyme suppression), this counts as semantic support.
