• Reduced lactate levels are proposed to decrease the stabilization of HIF-1α, thereby mitigating its effects on angiogenesis and metastasis.  
• Ultimately, this metabolic reprogramming is expected to improve the tumor’s responsiveness to sorafenib, a common therapeutic in HCC, with advanced metabolic flux assays and multi-omics analyses planned to validate this mechanism and therapeutic efficacy.
