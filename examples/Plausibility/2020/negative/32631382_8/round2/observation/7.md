1. Observation extraction:
 • Observation 1 (Abstract 1): In HCC, down‐regulation of MAT1A alongside up‐regulation of MAT2A and MAT2B is observed. Manipulation via miR‑203 modulates these genes, impacting SAM content, cell proliferation, apoptosis, migration, invasiveness, and stemness markers. This study implicates MAT2B’s oncogenic activity (linked to AKT activation) in tumor progression.
 • Observation 2 (Abstract 2): In HCC, the circular RNA circBNC2 is up‐regulated and acts as a molecular sponge for miR‑217, leading to overexpression of HMGA2. This in turn increases levels of glycolytic markers (HK2), proliferation markers (PCNA), and stemness factors (OCT4) and is associated with enhanced tumor growth and progression.
 • Observation 3 (Abstract 3): HCC exhibits metabolic reprogramming (the Warburg effect) driven by mTOR signaling. mTOR suppresses NEAT1_2 and paraspeckle formation; the release of RNA‑binding proteins then enhances mRNA splicing of key glycolytic enzymes. This results in increased glucose uptake, aerobic glycolysis, lactate production, and tumor growth.
