1. Hypothesis Summary:  
The hypothesis posits that in cholangiocarcinoma, cancer-associated fibroblasts (CAFs) remodel the tumor microenvironment by stiffening the extracellular matrix (ECM). This increased stiffness activates the YAP/TAZ signaling pathway in tumor cells through mechanotransduction. Once activated, YAP/TAZ is proposed to induce epigenetic modifications that enhance the stem-like properties of the cancer cells and contribute to their resistance to chemotherapy.
