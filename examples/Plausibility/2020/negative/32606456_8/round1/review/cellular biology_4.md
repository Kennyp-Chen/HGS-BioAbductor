1. Hypothesis Summary:
The hypothesis posits that in cholangiocarcinoma, cytokines secreted by cancer-associated fibroblasts (CAFs) work together with the metabolic changes driven by IDH mutations to induce epigenetic reprogramming in tumor cells. This combined effect is suggested to promote chemoresistance. Moreover, the hypothesis proposes that targeting or interrupting the molecular communication (“cross-talk”) between the CAFs and the cancer cells could reverse this resistance and restore chemosensitivity.

Hence, the hypothesis offers original insight that merits further investigation.
