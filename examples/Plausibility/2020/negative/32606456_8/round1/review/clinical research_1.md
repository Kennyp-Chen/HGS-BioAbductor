1. Hypothesis Summary  
The hypothesis suggests that in IDH-mutant intrahepatic cholangiocarcinoma, the oncometabolite 2‐hydroxyglutarate (2-HG) induces epigenetic changes that promote the overexpression of fibroblast-activating cytokines. These cytokines are proposed to reprogram the tumor microenvironment by enhancing the recruitment and activation of cancer-associated fibroblasts (CAFs). This CAF-driven remodeling is hypothesized to foster chemoresistance. Consequently, the hypothesis proposes that targeting the epigenetic machinery controlling cytokine production may disrupt the supportive stromal network and improve the response to chemotherapy.
