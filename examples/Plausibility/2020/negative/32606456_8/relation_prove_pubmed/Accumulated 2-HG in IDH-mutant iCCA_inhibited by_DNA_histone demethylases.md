# Search Strategy and Reasoning
I searched PubMed using the keywords: "2-hydroxyglutarate AND demethylases AND IDH-mutant AND cholangiocarcinoma." This directly targets the mechanistic relationship between accumulated 2-hydroxyglutarate (2-HG), produced by mutant IDH in intrahepatic cholangiocarcinoma (iCCA), and the inhibition of DNA/histone demethylases. The key was to find explicit mention that 2-HG inhibits demethylases in the context of IDH-mutant iCCA. The top result, PMID: 31827136, was fully reviewed for evidence in the abstract and full text.

# literature evidence
## "Mutant IDH1 confers resistance to energy stress in normal biliary cells through PFKP-induced aerobic glycolysis and AMPK activation." PMID: 31827136
"Mutant IDHs convert α-ketoglutarate (α-KG) to 2-hydroxyglutarate (2-HG), which affects the activity of multiple α-KG-dependent dioxygenases including histone lysine demethylases."
Further expanded in the full text:
"2-HG inhibits the family of α-KG–dependent dioxygenase enzymes, including lysine histone demethylases (KDM) and the ten-eleven translocation (TET) family of DNA hydroxylases."

Answer: True
