# Search Strategy and Reasoning
Initial queries used the minimal set of MeSH terms: "histone demethylase inhibition AND H3K27ac AND fibroblast cytokine". No direct results matched all concepts, so the search was broadened to "H3K27ac AND fibroblast cytokine" and then "histone demethylase inhibition AND H3K27ac". These queries yielded more results, including papers on histone acetylation marks (like H3K27ac), demethylase inhibition, and fibroblast-activating cytokines. Abstracts and full texts were reviewed for any sentence describing that inhibition of DNA/histone demethylases promotes increased deposition (or upregulation/enhanced presence) of the activating histone mark H3K27ac specifically at promoters of fibroblast-activating cytokine genes. Several papers discuss relevant processes but do not link demethylase inhibition to H3K27ac increase at cytokine promoters in fibroblasts. Review of fulltext for papers such as PMID: 35193410 and others for direct or implied evidence did not yield the specified relationship.
