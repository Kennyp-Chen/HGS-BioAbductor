## "The deficiency of ALKBH5 contributes to hepatic lipid deposition by impairing VPS11-dependent autophagic flux." PMID: 39434426
From the full text:
"ALKBH5 expression was markedly decreased in the livers of NAFLD patients and HFD-fed mice, as well as in PA-treated hepatocytes. In the NAFLD mouse model, the downregulation of ALKBH5 is tightly correlated with enhanced hepatic steatosis and metabolic stress. Mechanistically, we show that lipid overload/metabolic stress in NAFLD initiates ALKBH5 downregulation, which subsequently impairs autophagic flux, aggravating lipid deposition."
