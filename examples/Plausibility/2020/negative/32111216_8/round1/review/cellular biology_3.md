1. Hypothesis Summary  
─────────────────────────────  
The hypothesis proposes that in the liver, under hypoxic stress, abnormal m6A modifications occur in dendritic cells (DCs). This aberrant modification leads to impaired translation of lysosomal cathepsins, enzymes critical for antigen processing. As a consequence, antigen cross-presentation by these DCs is compromised, leading to defective T cell priming. The overall effect is an impaired immune response that facilitates immune evasion and ultimately contributes to the initiation of hepatocellular carcinoma.
