1. Hypothesis Summary
──────────────────────────────
The hypothesis proposes that when green tea is brewed at high temperatures, its catechins are converted into reactive quinone metabolites. These metabolites are suggested to selectively induce apoptosis (programmed cell death) in cancer stem cells, which are believed to be largely responsible for tumor recurrence and metastasis. The hypothesis further posits that this mechanism could be leveraged to complement standard cancer therapies, potentially enhancing treatment outcomes.
