# Search Strategy and Reasoning
The initial approach was to search PubMed using the key minimal concepts, omitting relationship language:
- Query: EGCG AND "cancer stem cells" AND self-renewal

This search yielded several direct hits specifically exploring EGCG and its effects on the self-renewal capacity of cancer stem cells (CSCs). The abstracts indicated that EGCG targets or inhibits CSC-associated properties, including self-renewal and stemness markers. To substantiate the evidence, the full text of the most directly relevant review (PMID: 29429153) was retrieved and analyzed, as it offers integrative molecular and experimental evidence rather than just broad speculation.
