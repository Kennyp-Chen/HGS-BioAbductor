1. Hypothesis Summary

• Second, through the direct inhibition of oncogenic signaling (with a focus on the PI3K/AKT cascade) and the concurrent modulation of cell cycle regulators (such as upregulation of p21waf1/Cip1 and downregulation of CDC25A), the therapy is intended to promote apoptosis in tumor cells.  
The hypothesis further incorporates computational modeling to optimize dosing and advanced delivery techniques to boost the precision and efficacy of the anti-tumor response.
