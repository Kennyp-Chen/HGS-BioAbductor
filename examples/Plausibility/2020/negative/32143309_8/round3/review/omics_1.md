1. Hypothesis Summary
──────────────────────────────
The hypothesis proposes that EGCG-rich green tea catechins can selectively target cancer stem cells (CSCs) by:
• Compromising mitochondrial oxidative phosphorylation through targeting unique mitochondrial membrane compositions and enzyme profiles found in CSCs.
• Concurrently modulating the IL-8 and PI3K/AKT signaling pathways, which are critical for the survival and self-renewal of CSCs.
This dual mechanism is expected to induce severe energy depletion and metabolic stress, thereby activating intrinsic apoptotic pathways and reducing the self-renewal capacity of CSCs.
