1. Hypothesis Summary

You propose that in hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC), chronic liver stress combined with oncogenic signals leads to an upregulation of m6A methyltransferases. This upregulation results in aberrant m6A modifications on tumor-suppressive long noncoding RNAs (lncRNAs). The modified lncRNAs undergo changes in their secondary structure that weaken or prevent binding to key epigenetic regulators, such as EZH2 and DNMTs. Consequently, the normal repression of metabolic and proliferative pathways fails, thereby facilitating cancer progression. Moreover, you put forward the idea that using targeted CRISPR-dCas13 RNA editing—validated through SHAPE-MaP structural profiling—to normalize m6A marks might restore the lncRNAs’ tumor-suppressive functions and impede HCC growth.
