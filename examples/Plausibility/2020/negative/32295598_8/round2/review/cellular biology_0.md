1. Hypothesis Summary:
You propose that in hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) cells under hypoxic conditions, increased reactive oxygen species (ROS) trigger an increase in selective m6A modifications on specific hypoxia-responsive long non-coding RNAs (lncRNAs; for example, lncRNA‐X). These m6A marks induce structural reconfigurations (i.e., conformational switches) that change the lncRNAs’ binding profiles. Specifically, they shift the interaction preference from proteins that normally stabilize HIF-1α mRNA to other proteins that instead promote HIF-1α mRNA degradation. The net result is reduced HIF-1α protein levels, which in turn leads to a decline in angiogenesis and metastasis. Your experimental strategy involves using advanced techniques including m6A-sequencing, CLIP-seq (to map protein–RNA interactions), and SHAPE-MaP (to analyze RNA structural dynamics) to validate this proposed pathway and explore its therapeutic potential.
