1. Observation extraction:
 • Abstract 1: m6A modifications occur widely in lncRNAs in HCC, affecting oncogene/tumor suppressor regulation and contributing to malignant behaviors. No specific link to ROS or to a reduction in HIF‐1α is mentioned.
 • Abstract 2: HCC displays a gain in m6A modifications on mRNAs that, through reduced YTHDF2, lead to altered decay of transcripts such as IL11 and SERPINE2. Hypoxia via HIF-2α is implicated in regulating YTHDF2—but this study centers on m6A’s effects on mRNAs, not lncRNAs.
 • Abstract 3: METTL3-mediated m6A modification increases on HIF-1α mRNA in HCC, with HBXIP driving this modification and promoting metabolic reprogramming and the malignant behavior of HCC cells.

In this case, the established explanation is more consistent with the observations; thus, the hypothesis does not offer a novel explanation.

Therefore, the causal pathways documented in the abstracts are explained by alternative mechanisms, and the hypothesis does not introduce a novel missing link for a subset of the observations.

This fundamental discrepancy—the hypothesis predicts a decline in HIF-1α protein levels while the observation shows upregulation—disproves the proposed mechanism.
