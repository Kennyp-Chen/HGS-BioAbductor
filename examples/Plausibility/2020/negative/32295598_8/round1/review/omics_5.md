1. Hypothesis Summary:
The hypothesis suggests that in hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC), oxidative stress increases m6A modifications on hypoxia-responsive long non-coding RNAs (lncRNAs). These modified lncRNAs exhibit enhanced interactions with RNA-binding proteins, which in turn stabilize HIF-1α mRNA. Elevated HIF-1α levels drive angiogenesis and establish a feedforward loop that further facilitates tumor progression in HCC.

• It offers an innovative perspective by implicating epitranscriptomic modifications as a mediator between environmental stress and transcription factor stabilization in the context of tumor progression.
