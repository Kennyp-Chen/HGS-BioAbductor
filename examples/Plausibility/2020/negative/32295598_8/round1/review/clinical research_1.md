1. Hypothesis Summary

The hypothesis proposes that in hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC), m6A RNA modifications act as a regulatory switch. This conversion is proposed to occur through altered binding interactions between the lncRNAs and epigenetic regulators, ultimately resulting in the silencing of tumor suppressor genes and contributing to cancer progression.
