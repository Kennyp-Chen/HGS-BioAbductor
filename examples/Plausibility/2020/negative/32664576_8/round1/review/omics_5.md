1. Hypothesis Summary  
──────────────────────────────  
The hypothesis posits that by selectively inhibiting NCOA4-mediated ferritinophagy in hepatocytes, the subsequent release of labile (free) iron will be reduced. This reduction, in turn, is expected to lower lipid peroxidation—a major driver of ferroptosis—and ultimately diminish both liver injury and fibrosis during the progression of nonalcoholic steatohepatitis (NASH). Essentially, the idea connects the modulation of an intracellular iron homeostatic mechanism (ferritinophagy) with the downstream mitigating effects on oxidative damage and tissue pathology in NASH.
