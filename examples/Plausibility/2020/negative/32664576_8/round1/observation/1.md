 • Observation 2: Multiple liver diseases (e.g., alcoholic liver injury, non‐alcoholic steatohepatitis, cirrhosis, and liver cancer) have been linked with ferroptosis.
 • Observation 3: Mitochondrial dysfunction—including enhanced reactive oxygen species production and compromised iron homeostasis—has been implicated as a contributor to ferroptosis in several contexts.

While targeting mitoNEET offers a specific angle—by mitigating mitochondrial iron overload—it does not replace the known broader mechanism.
