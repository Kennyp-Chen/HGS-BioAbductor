 • Observation 3 (Abstract 3): Ferroptosis in chronic liver disease (e.g., non‐alcoholic steatohepatitis) is fundamentally related to an imbalance in lipid peroxidation control, particularly when GPX4 activity is low and GSH is insufficient.
