# Search Strategy and Reasoning
I searched PubMed using the minimal keyword set "Labile iron pool AND Fenton reaction". The rationale is that the Fenton reaction underlies ROS production and is tightly linked mechanistically to the labile iron pool, so literature discussing both in one context is most likely to reflect evidence for the relationship.

Upon reviewing abstracts for direct or implied mitigation of the Fenton reaction (and ROS production) via reduction in the labile iron pool, several abstracts mention that increases in the labile iron pool drive the Fenton reaction and ROS production. To answer whether **reduction** in the labile iron pool mitigates the Fenton reaction/ROS, we need evidence explicitly stating that decreasing the labile iron pool leads to diminished ROS from the Fenton reaction.

One highly relevant article is PMID: 35476930 ("Cytochrome c oxidase mediates labile iron level and radioresistance in glioblastoma"), which describes that a decrease in the labile iron pool is associated with decreased lipid peroxidation, implicating lower ROS via the Fenton mechanism. I reviewed its abstract, where the direct mitigation effect is described.
