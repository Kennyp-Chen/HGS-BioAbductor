While this implies that glutathione supplementation (as GSH-Se nanozyme) can activate the GPX4 pathway (suggesting increased GPX4 activity/stability is promoted by glutathione), it does not specifically or explicitly state that *increasing endogenous glutathione synthesis* increases *GPX4 stability*. The evidence is indirect and based on supplementation, which is NOT equivalent to increased de novo synthesis.
