## "MALAT1 Long Non-Coding RNA: Functional Implications" (PMID: 32503170)
Exact sentence from the full text:
"Malat1 was also demonstrated to be induced in kidneys of hypoxic mice, and Malat1 was identified as one of the most upregulated non-coding transcripts upon hypoxia in a breast cancer cell line. ...
