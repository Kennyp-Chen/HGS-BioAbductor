1. Hypothesis Summary:

Specifically, during metabolic stress, this helix is proposed to orchestrate the localized assembly of glycolytic enzymes. Although it is not necessary for the basic proliferation of cells, the triple helix is thought to enhance metabolic plasticity by fine-tuning glycolytic flux when tumor cells encounter environmental challenges. The hypothesis further suggests that advanced techniques—such as CRISPR-based perturbation, high-resolution structural analyses, and single-cell metabolomics—can be employed to investigate how selective disruption of the triple helix (for instance, via small molecules) may reduce cancer cells’ metabolic adaptability, thereby inhibiting tumor progression while sparing normal cellular functions.
