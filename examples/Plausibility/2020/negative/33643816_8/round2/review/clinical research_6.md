Additionally, the hypothesis implies that this cycle can be therapeutically disrupted using advanced genomic editing tools (e.g., CRISPR/Cas-based RNA interference or antisense oligonucleotides) to restore mitochondrial function, normalize tumor bioenergetics, and improve responses to chemotherapy.
