This remodeling is proposed to disrupt gene regulatory networks that promote tumor growth. Simultaneously, these phytochemicals are suggested to trigger apoptotic signaling via mitochondrial lncRNAs, leading to dual outcomes: reprogramming tumor metabolism and enhancing the sensitivity of cancer cells to conventional chemotherapy.
