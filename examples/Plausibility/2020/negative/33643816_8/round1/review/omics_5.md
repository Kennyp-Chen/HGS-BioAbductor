1. Hypothesis Summary

This dual functionality is suggested to facilitate immune evasion in tumor cells. Consequently, the hypothesis further argues that a therapeutic strategy aimed at precisely targeting these dual-localized lncRNAs could restore antitumor immunity and lead to improved outcomes in cancers that are resistant to current immunotherapies.
