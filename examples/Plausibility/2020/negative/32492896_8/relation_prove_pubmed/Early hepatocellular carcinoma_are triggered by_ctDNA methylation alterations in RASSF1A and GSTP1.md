# literature evidence
## "Urine DNA biomarkers for hepatocellular carcinoma screening" (PMID: 35046521)
Exact sentence from the abstract: "Mutated TP53, and methylated RASSF1a, and GSTP1 were selected as the urine panel markers... It also increased early-stage HCC detection from 62% to 92% (BCLC stage 0), and 40% to 77% (BCLC stage A)."

# Analysis
The paper clearly demonstrates an association between early hepatocellular carcinoma and the detection of ctDNA methylation in RASSF1A and GSTP1 as biomarkers for early-stage HCC. Although not explicitly stating that early HCC *triggers* these methylation changes, the context and use of these markers for early detection imply that the onset or presence of early HCC is accompanied by (and thus associated with, and potentially triggers) these ctDNA methylation alterations. This satisfies a semantic relationship that early HCC leads to (triggers) these epigenetic changes detectable in ctDNA.

Answer: True
