## "The exosome encapsulated microRNAs as circulating diagnostic marker for hepatocellular carcinoma with low alpha-fetoprotein." (PMID: 32441313)
"Along with miR-21-5p, miR-10b-5p/miR-221-3p/miR-223-3p was found significantly upregulated in the exosome of HCC patients than CH/non-HCC. The comparable expression pattern was seen in anti-Asgr2 immuno-precipitated exosomes. ... ROC analysis showed that miR-10b-5p + miR-221-3p + miR-223-3p + miR-21-5p could differentiate CH/non-HCC(CH + LC) from HCC ... for low AFP-HCC vs CH/non-HCC ... sensitivity: 86%/86% and specificity:66%/53% for low AFP-HCC vs CH/non-HCC..."

This paper directly demonstrates that exosomes carrying miR-21 and miR-221 are secreted in significantly higher quantities as a result of early HCC (including in low AFP-HCC, which is a marker for early disease). The upregulation and specific presence in exosomes from HCC (and not from cirrhosis/chronic hepatitis) semantically supports the required relation of secretion as a result of early HCC.
