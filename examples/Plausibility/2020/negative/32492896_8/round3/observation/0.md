1. Observation extraction:
 • Abstract 1: AFP-producing liver cancer cells show a coordinated expression of 213 genes, including transcription factors and components of pathways such as Wnt and MAPK. Glypican 3—a component of the Wnt pathway—is strongly correlated with AFP levels.

There is no compelling evidence here that cytokine-mediated glycosyltransferase upregulation is the critical missing piece driving these effects.
