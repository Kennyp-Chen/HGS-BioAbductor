While both exosomal biomarkers and circulating microRNAs have been separately investigated in the context of HCC, merging TERT promoter methylation status in exosomes with microRNA profiles offers a novel angle. This approach seeks to harness the combined diagnostic potential of genetic/epigenetic markers and non-coding RNA, thus distinguishing itself from many established single-marker analyses. However, while innovative, the originality claim depends on clear differentiation from ongoing research that may be exploring multi-marker panels; explicit comparisons with the state-of-the-art studies will be necessary to underscore the novelty.
