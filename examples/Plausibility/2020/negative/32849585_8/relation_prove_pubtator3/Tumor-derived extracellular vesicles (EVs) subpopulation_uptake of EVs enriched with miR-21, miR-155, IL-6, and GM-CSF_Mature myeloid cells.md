# Search Strategy and Reasoning
I began by extracting the core MeSH-like terms: "extracellular vesicles" and "myeloid cells". Since the relationship of interest involved the uptake of EVs—specifically from tumors—enriched with miR-21, miR-155, IL-6, and GM-CSF by mature myeloid cells, I searched abstracts combining these terms alongside miRNAs, cytokines, and related signals. Relevant results were retrieved, consisting largely of review articles and original research showing that tumor-derived EVs modulate myeloid cells via miRNA transfer, but not specifically referencing uptake of EVs containing the exact combination (miR-21, miR-155, IL-6, GM-CSF) by mature myeloid cells.

Upon examining the top full abstracts:
- PMID: 38928399 did show evidence that melanoma-derived EVs can be taken up by myeloid cells and alter their function, mediated by distinct miRNAs. However, the miRNAs directly evidenced were miR-125a-5p, miR-125b-5p, and let-7e-5p, not miR-21 or miR-155.
- PMIDs: 37605155 and 32015297 described general roles of tumor-derived exosomal miRNAs modulating immune/myeloid cell function but did not specifically mention the uptake of EVs containing the full set of enriched factors (miR-21, miR-155, IL-6, GM-CSF) by mature myeloid cells.

Despite the general theme that tumor-derived EVs are taken up by myeloid lineage cells and can deliver signals and miRNAs, there was no specific, direct evidence for mature myeloid cells' uptake of tumor EVs enriched with miR-21, miR-155, IL-6, and GM-CSF.
