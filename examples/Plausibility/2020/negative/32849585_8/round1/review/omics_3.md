Your hypothesis posits that, in colorectal cancer, metabolites produced by the gut microbiota reprogram myeloid‐derived suppressor cells (MDSCs) to overexpress adenosine‐generating enzymes—specifically CD39 and CD73. This overexpression leads to an increase in adenosine-mediated immunosuppression, which in turn contributes to resistance to immunotherapy (e.g., checkpoint inhibitors). The hypothesis further suggests that targeting the microbiota–MDSC–adenosine axis could improve patient responses to these therapies.
