Thus, although the hypothesis posits a multi-organ cascade, most observed phenomena are adequately explained by conventional pathways.

Although intriguing, the observations from the abstracts already explain hepatic and systemic effects of PPAR agonists via established pathways (direct receptor activation, oxidative stress, and well-documented roles of different PPAR subtypes).
