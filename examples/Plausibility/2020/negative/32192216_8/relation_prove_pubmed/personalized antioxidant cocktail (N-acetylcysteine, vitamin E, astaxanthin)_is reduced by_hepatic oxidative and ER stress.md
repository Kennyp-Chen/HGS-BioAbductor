# Search Strategy and Reasoning
Initial search combined all relevant MeSH terms and drug names: "N-acetylcysteine AND vitamin E AND astaxanthin AND hepatic oxidative stress AND ER stress". No hits were found, so the search was refined by separating ER and oxidative stress terms, and also searched for combinations without the full set. The refined search "N-acetylcysteine AND vitamin E AND astaxanthin AND liver oxidative stress" yielded a key paper (PMID: 21764223) that discusses all three antioxidants and their impact on hepatic oxidative stress, though not as a cocktail and not in the context of ER stress specifically. Further review of the full text of this and related literature finds references to hepatic oxidative and ER stress alleviation by astaxanthin more commonly than by the cocktail, and an absence of evidence for all three antioxidants in a combined regimen targeting both oxidative and ER stress in the liver.
