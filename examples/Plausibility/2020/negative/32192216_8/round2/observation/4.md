1. Observation extraction:
 • Observation A: In rodents, hepatic PPARα activation (by chemicals such as fibrates or environmental contaminants) dramatically alters gene expression regulating lipid metabolism, and chronic activation is linked to liver cancer and steatosis.

 • Observation C: A variety of factors—including drugs, dietary changes, infections, and even microRNAs—are documented to modulate PPAR activity (both activation and suppression) in the liver.
 • Observation D: Current studies use genomics and gene expression profiles to identify direct chemical and nutrient-driven alterations of PPAR activity, with no report of an inter‐organ signaling axis mediated by exosomes or circadian-timed gene editing effects.
