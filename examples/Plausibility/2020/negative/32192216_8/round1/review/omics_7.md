1. Hypothesis Summary

The hypothesis posits that a finely tuned modulation of hepatic PPAR isoforms—specifically, the co‐activation of PPARβ/δ and PPARα together with a controlled, “moderated” level of PPARγ activity—results in a coordinated increase in both a specific lipid mediator (PC[18:0/18:1]) and the hepatokine FGF21. The downstream effect is an improvement in lipid profiles and insulin sensitivity, potentially mitigating the metabolic derangements seen in metabolic syndrome and non‐alcoholic fatty liver disease (NAFLD).
