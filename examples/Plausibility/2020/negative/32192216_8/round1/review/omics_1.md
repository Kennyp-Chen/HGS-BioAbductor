1. Hypothesis Summary  
─────────────────────────────  
The hypothesis proposes that circadian-regulated activation of hepatic PPARβ/δ triggers the secretion of exosomes loaded with specific microRNAs and the lipid molecule PC (18:0/18:1). These exosomes are then taken up by muscle cells, where they bring about epigenetic modifications that upregulate muscle PPARα. The upregulated PPARα subsequently enhances fatty acid oxidation in muscle, which may confer protection against metabolic disorders.

• Cross-talk Mechanism: The proposed liver-to-muscle communication mechanism via exosomes, particularly linked to circadian control, is relatively unexplored and offers an innovative perspective that could distinguish itself from current models of metabolic regulation.
