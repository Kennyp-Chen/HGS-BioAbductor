1. Observation extraction:
 • PPARs in general (PPARα, PPARβ/δ, PPARγ) are established regulators of lipid and glucose metabolism and inflammation in the liver.  
 • Abnormalities in PPARα, for example, are linked to hepatic steatosis and progression to steatohepatitis, fibrosis, and even liver cancer.  
 • PPARβ/δ is known to promote fatty acid β‐oxidation mainly in extrahepatic tissues.  
 • PPARγ, typically associated with lipid storage in adipocytes, is implicated in modulating hepatic triglyceride accumulation and disease evolution in NAFLD.  
 • Current therapeutic research uses PPAR agonists against metabolic and hepatic diseases, indicating known roles of these nuclear receptors in disease progression.
